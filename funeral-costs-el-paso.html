Funeral Costs in El Paso, Texas (2026)

By: Paul Paradis, Editor Updated: April 2026 State: Texas

El Paso's funeral market is shaped by something no other Texas city deals with at scale: cross-border Mexico repatriation. Roughly one in eight El Paso decedents are transported back to Chihuahua, Durango, or Zacatecas for burial, and the Sunset Funeral Home and Perches chapels have built their business around it. That specialization keeps local traditional-funeral pricing at $6,840 — about 5% below the Texas statewide average — but adds consular and transport line items other Texas cities never see.

Cross-Border Repatriation: What It Actually Costs

Returning remains to Mexico from El Paso involves consular documentation, embalming (required by Mexican law even when Texas doesn't require it), a hermetically sealed metal casket, and ground or air transport. Perches and Sunset both publish consular package pricing that typically runs $3,800–$5,500 for ground transport within 400 miles into northern Mexico, and $6,200–$8,400 for air transport to Mexico City, Guadalajara, or Monterrey. The Mexican consulate at 910 E. San Antonio Ave. issues the permiso de traslado — usually same-day if documentation is complete.

Your Rights in El Paso

FTC Funeral Rule Protection Every funeral home in El Paso must provide an itemized General Price List, let you choose only the services you want, and accept caskets purchased elsewhere. You are never required to buy a package. Read the full FTC Funeral Rule guide.

Texas Chapter 711 and Family Cemeteries in West Texas

Texas Health and Safety Code Chapter 711 permits family cemeteries on private land — a provision that still matters in the ranch country around El Paso County. Families in Hudspeth, Culberson, and outlying El Paso County can bury relatives on their own property with a permit and a plat filed with the county clerk. That option avoids cemetery plot costs entirely and remains legal across West Texas. Ask the Texas Funeral Service Commission for current plat-filing requirements before committing.

El Paso County Indigent Burial Assistance

El Paso County Commissioners Court operates an indigent burial program under Texas Health and Safety Code Section 694 — the program covers direct cremation or basic burial for decedents whose estates cannot pay, with families responsible only for documentation. Separately, TANF-eligible families may qualify for additional assistance through the Texas Health and Human Services Commission. The $255 Social Security lump-sum benefit and VA burial allowances at Fort Bliss National Cemetery stack with any county assistance.

Hidden Fees to Watch For in El Paso

Many families in El Paso encounter unexpected charges. Watch for these common hidden fees:

  • Casket handling fees — Illegal under the FTC Funeral Rule, but some providers still add them
  • Mandatory embalming claims — Texas does not require embalming in most circumstances. Know your consumer rights

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a funeral cost in El Paso?

A traditional funeral in El Paso averages $6,840 — about 5% under the $7,200 Texas statewide figure. Direct cremation lands near $1,615, cremation with a service runs $5,510, and a plot at Evergreen or Restlawn sits around $3,230. For cross-border repatriation services, Perches and Sunset typically quote $3,800–$5,500 for ground transport into Chihuahua or farther into northern Mexico.

Do more El Paso families choose cremation or burial?

Burial still edges out cremation in El Paso, tracking the Texas statewide 48%/52% cremation-to-burial split. Catholic tradition in the heavily Hispanic border community keeps burial rates higher than Austin or Houston. Where cremation is chosen, direct cremation at $1,615 remains the overwhelming cost driver — a traditional funeral plus plot at Martin or Perches pushes past $10,000 quickly.

Where does El Paso rank nationally for funeral pricing?

El Paso is one of the more affordable metro funeral markets in the U.S. — roughly 13% under the $7,848 national median for a traditional service. The Sun City's lower labor and real estate costs outweigh the modest transportation premiums that come with its geographic isolation from Houston and Dallas supplier networks.

Where can I compare funeral home prices in El Paso?

Phone or email Martin Funeral Home, Sunset Funeral Home, and Perches for written General Price Lists — the Texas Funeral Service Commission and the FTC Funeral Rule both require them to comply. The Funeral Consumers Alliance of South Texas covers El Paso County and maintains independent price-comparison worksheets. For cross-border services, compare two chapels with documented repatriation experience.

How much is direct cremation in El Paso?

About $1,615. Sunset and Perches both offer direct-cremation tiers beginning in the $1,400 range, while Martin Funeral Home typically lists its basic cremation closer to $1,750. The price covers transport, the cremation itself, a basic container, and return of the cremated remains — no viewing, no ceremony, and in Texas no embalming required.

How can El Paso families find low-cost funeral options?

Direct cremation at $1,615 sets the floor. El Paso County's indigent burial program under Texas H&S Code Section 694 covers qualifying low-income families. Chapter 711 family cemeteries on private land remain legal for Hudspeth and outlying El Paso County residents. Online casket retailers undercut funeral-home prices by 50–70%, and Fort Bliss National Cemetery provides free burial space for eligible veterans.
