Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026 · Maintained by Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor (Boston, MA).

We believe people dealing with a loss deserve to research sensitive topics without being profiled or marketed to. FuneralCostAnalyzer is an independent, one-person educational site run by Paul Paradis in Boston, Massachusetts, not a funeral home, not a lead-generation service, and not a marketplace. This policy explains, in plain language, what information is and is not collected when you visit the site, how it is used, your rights, and how to contact us with privacy requests.

What we collect and how we use it (summary)

  • What we collect from you directly: only what you type into the contact form (your name, email, subject, and message), the email address you enter in the optional email sign-up form, or what you send to us by email. We do not run accounts or logins.
  • What is collected automatically: standard server logs (IP, user agent, timestamp) by our host, Netlify; usage data gathered by Google Analytics on every page (described under Analytics below); and, if third-party ads are shown, cookies and identifiers by Google AdSense and similar networks.
  • How we use it: to respond to your message, to send the occasional email updates you signed up for, measure site traffic in aggregate, keep the site secure and operational, and (for third-party ad networks) to serve ads consistent with Google’s published policies.
  • What we do not do: no selling, renting, trading, or lead-generation sharing of reader data with funeral homes, insurers, or any third party; no retargeting we control; no profiling of individual visitors by us.
  • Your rights: CCPA/CPRA (California), GDPR (EEA/UK), and COPPA-relevant protections are described in the sections below. Email privacy@funeralcostanalyzer.com to exercise any of them.

Short version

  • No personal data is required to read the site. There are no accounts and no forced sign-ups; the email sign-up form is optional and asks only for an email address.
  • The free AI guide does not save your chat. The contact form only collects what you type into it.
  • The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages help readers, and it may display third-party ads through Google AdSense and similar networks. These third parties use cookies; opt-out links are provided below.
  • Your information is never sold, rented, or shared with funeral homes, insurance companies, or any other third party for lead-generation or marketing purposes.

Information this site does not collect

This site was designed with privacy as a priority. Visitors are often going through one of the most difficult times of their lives, and they deserve to research sensitive topics without first-party profiling.

  • No first-party personal-data collection. We do not ask for or store your name, address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information through normal use of this site. The only time personal data is received is when you voluntarily submit it through the contact form or the email sign-up form, or by emailing us.
  • No first-party advertising or retargeting cookies. We do not set our own advertising cookies or build marketing profiles of you. Third-party advertising networks (disclosed below) may set their own cookies; those are governed by each network’s policies, and opt-out tools are linked below.
  • No email harvesting. We do not collect email addresses through the site except the ones you type into the contact form or the optional email sign-up form, and we do not buy, scrape, or rent mailing lists.
  • No user accounts. There are no accounts, logins, or registration on this site.
  • No lead-generation or data sale. We do not collect reader information to match with funeral providers, insurance agents, or any other third party, and we do not sell, share, or rent reader data.

Analytics

Every page of this site runs Google Analytics (GA4, measurement ID G-Y3QZKG3K0Q) so that Paul can see how many people visit, which guides they read and where the site needs work. Google Analytics records the pages you view, your approximate location (country, region or city, worked out from your IP address), your device type, operating system and browser, the page or site that referred you, and a few events: clicks on outbound, affiliate and call-to-action links (the link address and the first 50 characters of its text) and form submissions (the form’s name and the page it was on). It does not receive your name, your email address or anything you type into a form or the AI chat. To tell repeat visits apart, it sets first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_* (the second one carries our measurement ID). We look only at aggregate reports, not at individual visitors. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, by blocking or deleting these cookies, or by using browser features that block tracking. Google handles this data under Google's Privacy Policy, and it explains how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

AI chat privacy

Our free AI guide lets you ask questions about funeral costs, options, and what to ask providers in a one-on-one chat. It is not a confidential service: your messages leave this site for processing, as explained below. Here is how that works:

  • Messages are processed by OpenAI. When you use the free AI guide, your messages are sent to OpenAI's API for processing. OpenAI's privacy policy governs how they handle that data.
  • We do not store your conversations. Your chat messages are not saved, logged, or recorded by us. We have no database of user conversations. To limit abuse, our server keeps short-lived request counts under a one-way hash of your IP address; the counts contain no message text.
  • Conversations are not saved between sessions. If you close your browser or leave the page, the conversation is gone. There is no chat history feature.
  • Do not share sensitive personal information. While we do not store your messages, we recommend not sharing Social Security numbers, financial account details, or other sensitive personal information in the chat as a general precaution.

Contact correspondence

If you choose to email us (for example, to share your experience, report a concern, or suggest a correction), we will receive and retain that correspondence only as long as needed to respond to your message and improve our content. We do not share, sell, or use your email correspondence for marketing purposes. We do not add you to any mailing list.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • Google’s advertising requirements are summarized by Google’s Advertising Principles. They are put in place to provide a positive experience for users.
  • Google’s handling of data in connection with advertising is governed by Google’s advertising and cookies policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Specifically, Google AdSense and other third-party ad networks may:

  • Use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet
  • Use the DoubleClick cookie to enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on your browsing patterns
  • Collect non-personally identifiable information (such as browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate geographic location) to deliver targeted advertising

You can also opt out of many third-party vendors' personalized-advertising cookies at aboutads.info/choices. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page or using the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool.

Advertising placements are selected by these third-party networks, not by this site’s editorial team. Ads do not constitute editorial endorsements, and we do not control which specific ads are shown to any individual visitor.

Advertising partners and your choices

This site may show display ads through Google or through an ad management partner such as Journey by Mediavine. These companies and their advertising partners may place and read cookies, use web beacons, and collect your IP address and similar identifiers to serve ads, limit how often you see an ad, and measure results. Ads may be personalized based on your visits to this and other sites.

  • How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: Google Ads Settings, Digital Advertising Alliance (US), Your Online Choices (EU).
  • Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google requires a Google-certified consent message before it shows personalized ads in these regions. Without your consent, only non-personalized or limited ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time through that message.
  • US residents of states with consumer privacy laws (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and others) can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. You can do this with a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser, which we honor where our ad partners support it, through Google Ads Settings, or by emailing privacy@funeralcostanalyzer.com.

Ads are chosen by these networks, not by our editors, and are not endorsements. We do not let advertisers target ads using health information you enter in our tools or chat.

Third-party services

This site uses a small number of third-party services to function. Here is what each one does and what data they may receive:

Google Fonts

We use Google Fonts to load the Inter and Merriweather typefaces. When your browser requests these fonts, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information (user agent string). Google's use of this data is governed by their privacy policy. We chose Google Fonts for reliability and performance, but want you to know this connection exists.

YouTube (Google) video on the home page

The home page includes one of our short videos from YouTube, a Google service, embedded in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Before you press play, the page shows only a thumbnail image served from Google’s i.ytimg.com, so Google receives your IP address and basic browser information for that image, as with Google Fonts. The YouTube player loads only when you press play. Once it does, YouTube may set cookies or use local storage on your device and may record that the video was played. That data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. You can also watch the video on YouTube directly instead.

Netlify hosting

This site is hosted on Netlify. Like all web hosting providers, Netlify maintains basic server logs that may include IP addresses, request timestamps, and pages visited. These logs are used for security and operational purposes. Netlify's handling of this data is governed by their privacy policy. We do not have access to individual visitor log entries.

Email sign-up form (Netlify Forms)

Some pages, including the home page, carry a short form headed “Get Our Free Funeral Cost Guide” that offers occasional email updates on funeral costs, consumer rights and planning ahead. The form asks for one thing, your email address; it also contains a hidden field that people should leave blank, which helps filter out automated spam. When you press the button, the form is submitted to Netlify Forms, a feature of our host, so Netlify receives and stores the submission on our behalf under its privacy policy. The page code is written to note that a form named “newsletter” was submitted, and on which page, and that event is sent to Google Analytics; the email address you entered is never included in it. The address is collected for the occasional updates the form offers. To stop the emails, or to have your address deleted from our records and from the stored form submissions, email privacy@funeralcostanalyzer.com from that address.

OpenAI API

The free AI guide feature sends your chat messages to OpenAI's API for processing. OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs in accordance with their privacy policy and terms of use. We encourage you to review their policies if you have concerns about how your messages may be handled on their end.

Cookies

The Google Analytics tag on every page sets the first-party cookies _ga and _ga_* to count visits, as described under Analytics above. Google AdSense and its partners set advertising cookies, also described above. Hosting and font services may also set essential cookies, and if you play the home page video, YouTube may set cookies or use local storage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the opt-out links above cover personalized ads.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13, and in keeping with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), we do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal information from children under 13. The content on this site is intended for adults who are making funeral and burial decisions or researching costs on behalf of their families.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information through this site (for example, via the contact form), please email privacy@funeralcostanalyzer.com and we will delete that information from our records. We also instruct the third-party services used on this site (Google AdSense, Google Analytics, YouTube, OpenAI, Netlify) to operate in ways consistent with COPPA and their own published policies.

Your rights under the GDPR (European Economic Area visitors)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We are committed to respecting these rights. Under the GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request that we correct any inaccurate personal data.
  • Erasure: Request that we delete your personal data, subject to certain legal obligations.
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict the processing of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Object: Object to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing purposes.
  • Data portability: Request a copy of your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format.

Because we collect minimal personal data through normal site use, most of these rights will not be applicable in practice. However, if you have submitted personal information to us through the contact form, the email sign-up form or email, you may exercise any of these rights by emailing privacy@funeralcostanalyzer.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

Legal basis for processing: Where we do process personal data (e.g, contact form and email sign-up submissions, or advertising cookies), our legal basis is either your explicit consent or our legitimate interest in operating this educational website and responding to inquiries.

Your rights under the CCPA (California residents)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) provide you with specific rights regarding your personal information. These rights include:

  • Right to know: You have the right to request that we disclose what personal information we have collected about you, the sources of that information, the purposes for collecting it, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to delete: You have the right to request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to opt out of the sale of personal information: We do not sell personal information. However, if our use of advertising cookies is considered a "sale" under the CCPA, you may opt out by adjusting your browser cookie settings or by using the opt-out links provided in the "Third-party advertising" section above.
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@funeralcostanalyzer.com. We will respond to verifiable consumer requests within 45 days as required by law.

Changes to this policy

When we change this privacy policy, we update this page and the “Last updated” date at the top of it. We encourage you to review this page periodically. The site runs Google Analytics, may show Google AdSense ads, and receives the contact and email sign-up forms described above; if we add another analytics, advertising or data-collection service, or change what these collect, we will describe it here before it goes live. The October 29, 2026 update described the email sign-up form, which earlier versions of this policy wrongly said did not exist, and corrected this paragraph, which referred to a date the page did not show and spoke of analytics as if it had not yet been introduced.
