Accuracy of Information

We make every effort to ensure that the funeral cost data and other information presented on this Site is accurate, current, and reliable. Our cost data is sourced from reputable organizations and publicly available resources, including:

  • The National Funeral Directors Association (NFDA) biennial cost surveys
  • The Funeral Consumers Alliance (FCA) consumer research and advocacy reports
  • State funeral regulatory boards and licensing agencies
  • Consumer price surveys and publicly available market data
  • The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Funeral Rule documentation

However, funeral costs vary significantly by geographic location, provider, time of year, and the specific services and products selected. The prices and cost ranges presented on this Site are general estimates based on national and regional averages. They may not reflect current prices in your specific area, and they should not be treated as quotes or guarantees.

We recommend obtaining itemized price lists directly from funeral homes in your area, as required by the FTC Funeral Rule, to get the most accurate and current pricing for your situation.
