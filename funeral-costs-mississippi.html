Funeral Costs in Mississippi: What Families Pay in 2026

By: Paul Paradis, Editor Last updated: October 22, 2026 Sources: NFDA, FCA, FTC Funeral Rule Region: South

Funeral costs in Mississippi run from about $1,400 for direct cremation up to $7,670 for a full traditional service with burial. The range is wide because funeral pricing in Mississippi is not standardized — what you pay depends heavily on which provider you call first.

What Drives Funeral Costs in Mississippi

Several forces shape funeral pricing in Mississippi. Regionally, the South trends toward lower-than-average costs relative to the $8,300 US median, and within Mississippi that gap widens between a dense metro like Jackson and the smaller towns — city prices routinely sit 10-30% higher.

In lower-cremation Mississippi areas the direct cremation market is thinner, so shopping around pays off more.

Your Consumer Rights in Mississippi

Know Your Rights Under the FTC Funeral Rule Every funeral home in Mississippi must comply with the Federal Trade Commission's Funeral Rule, which protects consumers nationwide.

If you believe a funeral home in Mississippi has violated these rights, you can file a complaint with the FTC at ftc.gov or contact the Funeral Consumers Alliance for guidance.

What's Specific to Mississippi (South)

In parts of Mississippi, corporate consolidation has pushed pricing up; in other parts, family-owned funeral homes still anchor the market.

Payment Assistance in Mississippi

  • If the family cannot pay, call the Mississippi county social services office before signing with a funeral home, since public help may require approval first and is often limited to the lowest-cost option.
  • Funeral homes in Mississippi usually report the death to Social Security, but the $255 lump sum still has to be claimed by the spouse or child (1-800-772-1213, within two years).
  • VA reimburses part of a Mississippi veteran's funeral through Form 21P-530EZ, filed by whoever paid; deaths not connected to service on or after October 1, 2026 qualify for up to $1,032 for burial and $1,032 for the plot, as our veteran burial benefits guide explains.
  • More options for Mississippi families, including Medicaid and charity help, are in our funeral payment assistance guide and how to pay for a funeral with no money.

Funeral rules and help in Mississippi

The Mississippi State Board of Funeral Service licenses funeral directors and establishments in Mississippi; call 601-932-1973 to check a license or file a complaint.

Mississippi is not on the Funeral Consumers Alliance list of nine states that restrict families from caring for their own dead. You can buy only the services you need, but someone must still file the Mississippi death certificate and get the permit.

Cremation timing: Mississippi sets no fixed waiting period before cremation, but a permit is needed first (15 Miss. Admin. Code Pt. 5, R. 4.7.2).

No verified statewide burial benefit is listed here for Mississippi. The county social-services or human-services office in Mississippi is the place to ask, and our assistance guide explains the federal options. Separately, Social Security pays Mississippi survivors $255 once, if they are an eligible spouse or child.

Eligible Mississippi veterans get a grave, opening and closing, a liner and a headstone at no cost in a national cemetery. In Mississippi, VA national cemeteries accepting burials: Biloxi National Cemetery (228-388-6668); Corinth National Cemetery (901-386-8311); Natchez National Cemetery (601-445-4981); state veterans cemeteries: Mississippi Veterans Memorial Cemetery - Kilmichael (662-262-4232); Mississippi Veterans Memorial Cemetery - Newton (601-683-2959). Source: VA National Cemetery Administration.

Next steps in Mississippi

  1. Get written price lists from at least two Mississippi providers before you agree to anything.
  2. Consider a Mississippi memorial service after cremation or burial, which avoids embalming and viewing fees.
  3. Ask your Mississippi county human-services office about indigent burial help before you commit.
  4. Veterans' families can schedule through the VA's National Cemetery Scheduling Office; Biloxi National Cemetery is listed above.
  5. Report problems with a provider to the licensing board at 601-932-1973.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average price of a funeral in Mississippi right now?

Current averages in Mississippi come in at $5,900 for a traditional funeral, $4,800 for cremation with a service, and from $1,400 for direct cremation — with the traditional figure sitting 29% below the $8,300 US median. Cemetery and burial fees add roughly $2,600 when applicable.

Does cremation save money compared with burial in Mississippi?

A direct cremation in Mississippi runs about $1,400, whereas a traditional funeral with burial averages $5,900 before adding the plot — so the gap can easily exceed $4,500. Mississippi's cremation rate of 28% sits below the roughly 63% national figure.

What are my consumer rights at Mississippi funeral homes?

Under the FTC Funeral Rule (16 CFR 453), every Mississippi funeral home must give you an itemized General Price List, quote prices by phone, and accept a casket or urn you bought elsewhere without a handling fee. According to the Funeral Consumers Alliance, Mississippi does not require families to hire a funeral director for every step. You can confirm a Mississippi license or file a complaint with the Mississippi State Board of Funeral Service at 601-932-1973.

How do I keep funeral costs down in Mississippi?

Three moves cut the most: compare at least three Mississippi General Price Lists side by side before choosing a provider, strip packages down to only the services you actually want, and choose direct cremation ($1,400) if cost is the priority.

Is embalming legally required in Mississippi?

No — embalming is rarely required by Mississippi law.

How do funeral costs in Mississippi compare to other states?

The state's traditional-funeral average of $5,900 sits 29% below the $8,300 national median, and the South region overall trends toward moderate to lower pricing than the rest of the country.

Which funeral option costs the least in Mississippi?

Direct cremation carries the lowest price tag in Mississippi, from roughly $1,400. Direct burial is the next step up at about $2,950 and is still considerably cheaper than a traditional service.

Where do I look for low-cost funeral providers in Mississippi?

Start with GPL requests from at least three Mississippi funeral homes — that alone exposes the outliers.
