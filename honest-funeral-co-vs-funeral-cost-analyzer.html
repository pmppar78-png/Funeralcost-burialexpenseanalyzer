    Honest Funeral Co. vs Funeral Cost Analyzer (2026)

    By Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor · November 19, 2026

    Honest Funeral Co., at honestfuneral.co, is an independent consumer site that helps families with funeral choices, costs, and paperwork. A live load of The Posted Funeral Price Report, October 2026 on November 19, 2026 showed prices read from funeral homes' own websites between October 14 and 18, 2026, in a corrected edition dated November 10, 2026. The report describes what 1,079 price lists show. Of 5,869 licensed-home websites they could read in full, 26% showed prices. On a typical list, the lowest direct cremation price was $2,200 (938 lists), the lowest immediate burial price was $2,995 (865 lists), and the basic services fee was $2,255 (991 lists).

    Their sitemap on that day listed this October report and no earlier or later posted-price report, and a fetch of /reports returned HTTP 404. We therefore describe the October 2026 edition we actually loaded, not a monthly series. They also publish ZIP-adjusted modeled estimates, city pages, and tools such as a price-list checker.

    Why readers choose us over Honest Funeral Co.

    Like Honest Funeral Co., this site is free, does not require a sign-up, distinguishes estimates from prices that come off a posted list, cites the FTC Funeral Rule, and publishes a methodology. Neither of us is a funeral home.

    • A 50-state index with official NFDA medians on their own labeled lines. Our National Funeral Cost Index holds the NFDA 2023 medians of $8,300 and $6,280 apart from our 2026 state-estimate averages ($7,736 traditional, $6,072 with a cremation service, $1,834 direct cremation, $3,656 for a plot). Honest Funeral Co. also separates estimates from posted-list figures; what we add is that single, downloadable 50-state table and PDF.
    • A free embeddable widget tied to that index. The funeral cost widget shows the same figures, with source and year labels, and can load updates from a hosted file. We did not find a comparable embeddable index widget on honestfuneral.co when we checked on November 19, 2026.
    • Named, home-by-home posted lists in eight cities. We transcribe line items from public General Price Lists in New York City, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Phoenix, Dallas, San Diego, and Austin, with dates and source links. The October report we loaded says it names no funeral home. Their ZIP page is presented as their estimates, not funeral homes' prices. That is a real difference in what a reader can open and check, even though their posted-price sample is much larger than ours.

    Where Honest Funeral Co. still leads

    Their October 2026 posted-price sample is far larger than our eight-city notes, and they are frank about how many homes still do not post a list, how old some posted lists are, and how often nearby homes disagree on direct cremation. The price-list checker, bill tools, and ZIP-adjusted modeled ranges are tools we do not have. Their homepage states that they take no money from funeral homes and that price information is free; we will not talk around that policy. Readers who want a wide view of prices that homes actually posted online, or help reading a list they were handed, should use those pages and then confirm the current list with the home.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does Honest Funeral Co. publish a monthly price report?

    When we checked on November 19, 2026, their sitemap listed one posted-price report: The Posted Funeral Price Report, October 2026, with prices read October 14 to 18, 2026, and a corrected edition dated November 10, 2026. A /reports index was not found (HTTP 404). We do not describe that as a monthly series unless later editions appear.

    Do they name individual funeral homes in the October report?

    The October 2026 report states that it names no funeral home and that it shows no figure from fewer than six price lists. Their ZIP lookup page is labeled as their estimates, not funeral homes' prices. We still publish a small, dated sample of named homes in eight cities, with source links, which is a different and much smaller product than their posted-price sample.

    Which figures should I use for planning?

    Use a current General Price List for a decision. Honest Funeral Co. is stronger when you want a large sample of prices that homes posted online, or a ZIP-adjusted modeled range. Use our National Funeral Cost Index when you want official NFDA 2023 medians and our 50-state estimates held on separate, labeled lines, with a CSV, a PDF, and an embeddable widget.
