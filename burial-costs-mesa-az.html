Burial Costs in Mesa, Arizona (2026)

By: Paul Paradis, Editor Updated: April 2026 State: Arizona

Burial in Mesa runs against the East Valley's prevailing wind: Arizona as a whole now buries fewer than one in three of its dead, and Maricopa County is particularly cremation-heavy. That market context matters because the Mesa funeral homes and cemeteries that still handle a meaningful traditional-burial caseload tend to price it carefully. Expect a 2026 Mesa burial to total between $14,343 and $20,181 once every line item is settled.

The LDS Footprint in the East Valley

Mesa's religious demographics shape burial pricing in ways most Arizona pricing guides overlook. The East Valley has one of the highest concentrations of LDS families in the country, and the cultural preference for traditional burial over cremation holds steady even as the broader Arizona rate tips toward cremation. This sustained burial demand supports consistent plot turnover at Mesa City Cemetery and the larger private memorial parks along the Superstition corridor, and it keeps funeral homes like Bunker's Garden Chapel and Meldrum Mortuary experienced in traditional service pricing. Ask specifically whether section pricing reflects current or legacy rates.

Burial Formats Available in Mesa

  • Traditional burial — Viewing, service, casket, vault, interment. Still dominant among East Valley LDS, Catholic, and older Anglo families.
  • Direct burial — Burial shortly after death with no viewing, embalming, or facility ceremony. Nearly every Mesa funeral home publishes this rate.
  • Green burial — Available at a handful of Arizona grounds; fewer options within Maricopa County proper than in Sedona or Tucson.
  • Mausoleum entombment — Offered at most private memorial parks in the East Valley; base pricing higher than ground burial but skips the vault.

Using Arizona's Cremation-Heavy Market as Leverage

With cremation at about 68 percent of Arizona dispositions, Mesa providers that still focus on burial sometimes have pricing flexibility they do not advertise. Mentioning direct cremation as your alternative during a quote conversation occasionally surfaces more favorable burial pricing than the published General Price List suggests.

Casket sourcing: Under the FTC Funeral Rule, Mesa funeral homes must accept a casket from any third-party retailer with no handling fee. Compare online casket retailers.

Where Mesa Families Actually Save

  • Compare Mesa City Cemetery directly against a private memorial park. Public and private pricing diverge significantly; getting both itemized sheets side by side usually reveals a $1,000 to $2,000 plot difference.
  • Ask about hybrid or natural sections. Several East Valley cemeteries have quietly added sections that skip the vault and embalming requirements.
  • Unbundle the casket. 50 to 70 percent savings are typical. Casket buying guide.
  • Order the marker from a monument dealer. Mesa and Gilbert monument companies cut stones for less than cemetery in-house vendors. Headstone cost guide.
  • Consider Arizona National Cemetery in Phoenix for veterans. Free burial for eligible veterans and spouses; see veteran burial benefits.

Common Questions

How much does burial cost in Mesa?

A Mesa traditional burial totals between $14,343 and $20,181 in 2026. Funeral service near $7,560, plot $3,570, casket $2,268, vault $1,428, opening and closing $1,785, and marker $1,500 to $3,000. East Valley LDS demand keeps traditional-burial pricing at the municipal and private memorial parks fairly stable year to year.

Is a burial vault required in Mesa?

Arizona law does not require an outer burial container. Most Mesa cemeteries require one by internal policy to prevent ground settling. A concrete grave liner typically meets the rule at roughly half the cost of a sealed vault. Confirm with the specific cemetery — Mesa City Cemetery and the private East Valley parks may differ in their policies.

What are the cheapest burial options in Mesa?

Direct burial through Bunker's Garden Chapel, Meldrum Mortuary, or a comparable Mesa funeral home eliminates embalming and facility fees. Mesa City Cemetery usually prices plots below the private memorial parks nearby. Arizona's hybrid and natural burial sections, where available, run 30 to 50 percent below traditional sections in the same cemetery. Eligible veterans should look at Arizona National Cemetery in Phoenix for free interment.

How do Mesa burial costs compare to Arizona?

Mesa runs roughly 5 percent above the statewide average — $7,560 funeral service versus $7,200, $3,570 plot versus $3,400. Phoenix prices slightly higher than Mesa; Tucson typically matches the statewide average. The East Valley's sustained traditional-burial demand keeps pricing steadier here than in Arizona's cremation-dominant urban cores.
