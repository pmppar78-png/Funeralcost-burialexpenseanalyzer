    Real Funeral Home Prices in Chicago

    By Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor · November 19, 2026

    This page reports General Price List figures from five Chicago funeral homes that posted a list we could read. The figures were recorded on November 19, 2026 and appear here as printed, including ranges.

    Three of the five lists are official Dignity Memorial location pages dated September 22, 2026. Malec & Sons and Ridge share many of the same itemized prices on that date; they are still separate Chicago addresses, so each has its own row. Barr Funeral Home's list is dated May 4, 2025, older than the others in this sample.

    This is not a complete map of Chicago funeral homes. Request a current list from any home you are considering, using the request letter and phone script.

    How to read these figures

    • Richard-Midway Funeral Home. Direct cremation with a casket selected from the home is printed at $2,725; with an alternative container or a container provided by the client is $3,175, as published. A per-day visitation line of $950 is also listed.
    • Barr Funeral Home. Direct cremation is $3,775 on every container option printed, including a container provided by the purchaser. Immediate burial is the published package price of $3,375. This list is older than the 2026 lists in this sample.
    • Malec & Sons Funeral Home. Official Dignity Memorial location price list. The lowest direct cremation line printed is $3,295, including the purchaser-container / casket-from-facility line. Immediate burial with a purchaser container is $4,795.
    • Ridge Funeral Home. Official Dignity Memorial location price list. Several itemized prices match Malec & Sons on the same date; the two homes are separate Chicago locations and are recorded separately.
    • Drake & Son Funeral Home. Official Dignity Memorial location price list. The lowest direct cremation line printed is $5,290. Immediate burial with a purchaser container is $6,490.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do two Chicago Dignity homes show the same direct-cremation range?

    Malec & Sons and Ridge Funeral Home posted lists on September 22, 2026 with the same direct-cremation and immediate-burial ranges. They are different locations, so both rows stay in the table, each with its own source link.

    Is Barr Funeral Home's 2025 list still useful?

    It is the list Barr still posts, and we recorded it with its May 4, 2025 effective date. It may have changed since then. Ask the home for the list in force when you call.

    Does this page replace a Chicago state estimate?

    No. Our Illinois planning estimate is a statewide figure. These five lists are a local sample of posted prices. Use both as context, then compare current General Price Lists.
