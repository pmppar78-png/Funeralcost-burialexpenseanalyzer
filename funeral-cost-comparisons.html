    Funeral Cost Analyzer Compared: NFDA, Parting, Funeralocity and Others

    By Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor · November 19, 2026

    This hub gathers factual, dated comparisons between Funeral Cost Analyzer and other sites families meet when they search for funeral or cremation costs. Each child page was written after a live check on November 19, 2026. We describe what the other site offers, what we offer, and where we still cannot match them honestly.

    We do not use their logos, we do not call ourselves number one, and we do not invent local funeral-home prices to close a gap. Where they lead, the page says so in plain language.

    What you get here that other sites don't offer

    This site is free, requires no account, and publishes funeral and cremation planning figures for all 50 states, a dated National Funeral Cost Index, calculators, and a free AI guide. You can also download a fill-in end-of-life planner and keep it yourself.

    Nothing you write in the planner is seen or stored by us: you download it, fill it in, and keep the file yourself.

    Tools for comparing prices yourself

    A ZIP or state lookup shows our published 2026 planning estimates for the state that matches your ZIP. A General Price List request letter and phone script helps you ask a funeral home for its current itemized prices. The planning kit gathers the checklist, worksheet, letter, calculator, and embeddable widget in one place. The site also has a national index, a printable checklist, and a free AI guide.

    Why we focus on state-level prices

    Funeral homes can change their prices at any time, so a list of local prices can be out of date the day after it's published. A realistic state-level estimate built from published sources still helps you walk in prepared whether the final bill comes in a little lower or a little higher. That is why our state estimates are the baseline, and why our small city samples show each list's effective date and a link to the home's current list.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is Funeral Cost Analyzer affiliated with NFDA, Parting, or these other sites?

    No. We are an independent educational site. We cite NFDA public figures accurately and we link to other services when they do a job we cannot, such as showing a funeral home General Price List. We do not claim their endorsement.

    Do you have real local funeral-home prices?

    We publish state planning estimates and tools for requesting a General Price List, and a small, dated sample of lists that funeral homes posted in eight cities, each linked to the home's own list. That is not a ZIP-level database. Parting.com and Funeralocity still lead on large provider directories.

    When were these comparisons checked?

    The competitor features described here were checked on the live sites on November 19, 2026. We will date any later revision.

    Why are there separate pages for each competitor?

    Each page is written for people comparing a specific service with this site, including alternative and versus searches. This hub is the table of contents. None of the pages claims to be number one, and each notes where the other site still leads.
