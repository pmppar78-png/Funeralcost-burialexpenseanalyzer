    Everplans and Funeral Cost Analyzer: What Each Is For (2026)

    By Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor · November 19, 2026

    Everplans is not primarily a funeral-cost table. A live load of everplans.com on November 19, 2026 described a place to keep "all the pieces of your world," with sections for home life, digital organization, estate planning, and end-of-life planning, plus paid plans and an Android app. A /tools path returned 404; /checklists loaded a topic-category browse page.

    We cannot honestly copy a login vault, a mobile app, or an estate-document product in a short on-site build, and we should not bolt one on as a thin imitation. What we can offer, and now package in one place, is a free planning kit: checklists, a worksheet, a General Price List request letter, calculators, and state estimates, with no account required.

    Where Everplans still leads

    If you want a single login to store documents, insurance, and end-of-life wishes, Everplans is that kind of product. We do not offer it, and a static checklist is not the same thing.

    Where this site is the better cost tool

    If you want 2026 funeral and cremation planning figures, official NFDA medians cited with dates, or a letter to request a General Price List, those are our pages. You can use them without creating an account.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is Funeral Cost Analyzer an Everplans alternative?

    It can be an alternative for funeral cost research and a printable planning packet. It is not an alternative for a document vault, an app, or paid estate-organization software.

    Do I need an account here?

    No. The checklists, calculators, state pages, letter, and AI guide work without signing in. An email form exists only if you want a note when the printable checklist is revised.

    Why did you not compare every Everplans article?

    A funeral-cost article URL we tried returned 404 on November 19, 2026. The comparison uses the live homepage and the checklists section we could open, and it does not invent content we could not see.
