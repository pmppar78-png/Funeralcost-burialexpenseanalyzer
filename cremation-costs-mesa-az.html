Cremation Costs in Mesa, Arizona (2026)

By: Paul Paradis, Editor Updated: April 2026 State: Arizona Cremation Rate: 68%

Mesa's cremation market is shaped by one demographic reality most East Valley guides skip: the LDS footprint. Bunker Family Funerals has operated in Mesa since 1931 and remains the dominant provider for the city's substantial Latter-day Saint community, while Meldrum Mortuary and Mariposa Gardens handle the non-LDS and secular side of the East Valley. Direct cremation averages $1,680 — slightly above the Arizona state median — with the $80 gap largely reflecting the full-service competitive pressure Bunker brings to the category.

National context: the NFDA 2024 Cremation & Burial Report and the CANA 2024 Annual Report put the U.S. cremation rate near 60.5 percent, and Mesa providers operate under the oversight of the Arizona State Board of Funeral Directors and Embalmers. Families ordering death certificates typically deal with the Maricopa County Department of Public Health Office of Vital Registration.

Arizona's Refrigeration-in-Lieu-of-Embalming Rule

Arizona statute allows refrigeration as a substitute for embalming for a period up to seven days — a provision the state Board of Funeral Directors and Embalmers enforces, and one that substantially lowers Mesa cremation pricing versus states requiring embalming before even short holds. When Bunker Family Funerals or Meldrum quote $1,680 direct cremation, no embalming line is embedded in that figure because the remains are held under refrigeration between pickup and the retort. Families who want visitation before cremation still need embalming; families on the direct cremation path do not.

Bunker Family Funerals and Mesa's LDS Funeral Culture

Bunker Family Funerals is woven into Mesa's LDS community in a way no other East Valley provider is. Latter-day Saint funeral practice has historically leaned toward traditional burial, but Mesa's younger LDS families have moved toward cremation over the last decade, and Bunker's cremation-with-service packages have grown accordingly. Their pricing on the full-service cremation path — landing near the $6,090 figure — reflects the LDS expectation of chapel visitation, family prayer, and sometimes a separate graveside committal of cremated remains at Mesa City Cemetery or the Mesa Arizona Temple grounds (though Temple burial itself is not permitted).

Maricopa County Indigent Services

Maricopa County runs a formal indigent cremation program administered through the Human Services Department. The program covers direct cremation only — no service, no urn upgrade — and selects contracted providers via bid. Mesa families who qualify are typically routed to one of the contracted crematories rather than to Bunker or Meldrum, and the eligibility threshold is narrow: documented indigency at the time of death, estate assets under a statutory minimum, and no next of kin capable of assuming the cost. Applications go through Maricopa County Social Services.

Scattering on the Sonoran Desert

Arizona's scattering landscape is unusually generous. Tonto National Forest, right against Mesa, allows scattering with a free dispersal permit. Saguaro National Park near Tucson requires a Superintendent's permit. Usery Mountain Regional Park, Lost Dutchman State Park, and the Bush Highway stretch along the Salt River all accommodate scattering with written authorization. Families often choose Saguaro Lake or Apache Lake because the water scatter falls cleanly under private-charter protocols rather than EPA marine rules.

Reader Questions

How much does cremation cost in Mesa?

Mesa direct cremation averages $1,680 through Bunker, Meldrum, Mariposa Gardens, and the smaller East Valley houses. Full-service cremation with chapel visitation averages $6,090. The Mesa figure runs $80 above the Arizona state median, reflecting the competitive pressure Bunker brings to the category on the LDS-traditional side of the market. Arizona's refrigeration-in-lieu-of-embalming rule keeps direct-cremation pricing efficient.

What types of cremation are available in Mesa?

All three standard formats run through Mesa providers. Direct cremation at $1,680 is the simplest, with no service and no visitation. The mid-tier format pairs cremation with a memorial service held at the family's LDS ward building or chapel. Bunker's full-service cremation at $6,090 includes chapel visitation, family prayer, and often a separate graveside committal of cremated remains at Mesa City Cemetery.

Where can I compare cremation prices in Mesa?

Start by calling three providers in Mesa and requesting their General Price List in writing, which the FTC Funeral Rule (16 CFR Part 453) requires every funeral home to provide on request. Compare the direct-cremation line item and basic-services fee side by side. Parting.com, the Funeral Consumers Alliance, and local Better Business Bureau listings offer additional price points for Mesa-area providers.

Can I scatter ashes in Mesa?

Tonto National Forest right against Mesa allows scattering with a free dispersal permit. Usery Mountain Regional Park, Lost Dutchman State Park, and the Salt River's Bush Highway stretch accommodate scattering with written authorization. Saguaro Lake and Apache Lake are popular water-scatter locations and fall under private charter rather than EPA marine rules, meaning permits are straightforward.
