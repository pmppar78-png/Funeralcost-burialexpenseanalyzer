About the Funeral Cost & Burial Expense Analyzer

We are an independent educational resource dedicated to helping families understand funeral costs, compare their options, and know their consumer rights — before making financial commitments during one of the most difficult periods of their lives.

Our mission

Losing someone you love is one of the hardest experiences a person can go through. In the middle of grief, families are often asked to make significant financial decisions on a short timeline — decisions about funeral services, burial or cremation, caskets, urns, cemetery plots, and more. Many families report feeling overwhelmed, pressured, or uncertain about whether the prices they were quoted were fair.

We created the Funeral Cost & Burial Expense Analyzer to change that. Our goal is simple: give every family the information they need to make informed decisions with clarity, confidence, and dignity. We believe that understanding what things cost — and what your rights are — is one of the most empowering steps you can take during a difficult time.

What we do

We research, organize, and present funeral cost data from across the United States so that families can quickly understand what to expect. Our resources include:

  • Cost data for all 50 states: Average funeral, cremation, and burial costs broken down by state and major city, based on publicly available consumer surveys and industry data from the National Funeral Directors Association (NFDA) and the Funeral Consumers Alliance (FCA).
  • A funeral cost estimator: An interactive calculator that helps families get a ballpark estimate based on their region, service type, and preferences.
  • Consumer rights guides: Plain-language explanations of the FTC Funeral Rule, General Price Lists, and your legal right to compare prices and choose only the services you want.
  • Payment assistance resources: Information about Social Security death benefits, veteran burial benefits, Medicaid assistance, crowdfunding, and other ways to manage funeral expenses.
  • Planning guides: Practical, step-by-step guides for topics like prepaid funeral plans, funeral insurance, green burial options, body donation, and funeral planning checklists.
  • An AI-powered helper: A conversational assistant that can answer common questions about funeral costs, help organize your thoughts, and point you toward relevant resources — all without collecting or storing your personal information.

Who we are

We are a small team of researchers, writers, and consumer advocates with backgrounds in personal finance, consumer protection, and public health communications. We are not affiliated with any funeral home, casket manufacturer, insurance company, or other commercial entity in the funeral industry.

Our editorial team researches and writes every piece of content on this site. We cross-reference multiple data sources, review publicly available General Price Lists, and consult primary regulatory documents to ensure accuracy. Our full methodology is described on our Editorial Standards & Methodology page.

Our values

Independence

No funeral home, insurance company, casket manufacturer, or other commercial entity has any influence over what we write or how we present information. While some pages on this site contain affiliate links (clearly disclosed), these relationships never affect our editorial content, cost data, or recommendations. Our primary obligation is to the families who use this resource. Learn more in our Editorial Standards.

Accuracy

Every cost figure, legal reference, and factual claim on this site is sourced from credible, publicly available data — primarily NFDA surveys, FCA publications, FTC regulatory documents, and state funeral board reports. We review and update content regularly. When we discover an error, we correct it promptly.

Compassion

We write for people who may be reading through grief, stress, or time pressure. We use plain language, avoid jargon, and never pressure or alarm our readers. We present facts and options — not arguments or sales pitches.

Privacy

We believe families navigating loss deserve complete privacy. We do not track our visitors with advertising cookies or build marketing profiles. Our Privacy Policy explains in detail what data we collect (very little) and what we do not.

Transparency

We disclose our data sources, our research methodology, our affiliate relationships, and the limitations of our information. If we do not know something, we say so. If data is uncertain or varies significantly, we present the range and explain why.

What we are not

It is important to be clear about what this site does not do:

  • We are not a funeral home. We do not arrange, conduct, or sell funeral services of any kind.
  • We are not a marketplace. We do not connect buyers and sellers of funeral services or accept payment to list or promote funeral providers.
  • We are not financial, legal, or medical advisors. Our content is educational. We always recommend consulting with licensed professionals for decisions specific to your situation.
  • We are not a lead generation service. We do not sell your contact information to funeral homes, insurance companies, or any third party.

Why trust this resource

We understand that trust must be earned, especially on a topic this sensitive. Here is how we work to earn yours:

  • Cited sources: Our cost data comes from NFDA surveys, FCA publications, FTC regulations, state funeral regulatory boards, and publicly available General Price Lists. We attribute our sources throughout the site.
  • Editorial standards: We maintain detailed editorial standards that govern our research process, content review cycle, and correction policy.
  • No hidden agendas: We do not accept payment to feature, promote, or suppress any editorial content. Our affiliate relationships are disclosed transparently.
  • Regular updates: We review all content and cost data on a regular schedule, with every page carrying a "last reviewed" date. Our most recent comprehensive site review was completed in March 2026.
  • Open feedback: If you spot an error or have a suggestion, we invite you to contact us. We take corrections seriously and respond to every message.

Our commitment to families

Every piece of content on this site is written with one question in mind: Does this help a family make a more informed decision during one of the hardest moments of their life?

If the answer is yes, we publish it. If not, we rework it until it does. That is our standard, and it is one we hold ourselves to on every page.
