Payment Assistance in South Dakota

  • If the family cannot pay, call the South Dakota county social services office before signing with a funeral home, since public help may require approval first and is often limited to the lowest-cost option.

Funeral rules and help in South Dakota

Funeral homes in South Dakota are licensed by the South Dakota State Board of Funeral Service (605-642-1600). That office takes South Dakota complaints about pricing, misleading sales, or a home that will not hand over its price list.

South Dakota is not on the Funeral Consumers Alliance list of nine states that restrict families from caring for their own dead. You can buy only the services you need, but someone must still file the South Dakota death certificate and get the permit.

For families comparing a funeral with cremation, note that South Dakota requires a 24-hour wait before cremation (S.D. Codified Laws § 34-26A-13.1).

South Dakota help with funeral costs, where it exists, is usually run by counties, and we could not confirm a statewide figure. Start with the South Dakota county social-services office; our public assistance guide covers what to ask. The federal Social Security lump sum available in South Dakota is $255, paid only to a qualifying spouse or child.

A South Dakota veteran's grave in a national cemetery costs the family nothing for the plot, opening and closing, or marker. In South Dakota, VA national cemeteries accepting burials: Black Hills National Cemetery (605-347-3830); state veterans cemeteries: South Dakota Veterans Cemetery (605-277-8094); Akicita Owicahe Lakota Freedom Veterans Cemetery (605-455-1497); Sicangu Akicita Owicahe Veterans Cemetery (605-259-3044). Source: VA National Cemetery Administration. The Sicangu Akicita Owicahe Veterans Cemetery number comes from the tribe's office directory.

  1. Ask every South Dakota provider for its General Price List; the FTC Funeral Rule says they must give it to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a funeral cost in South Dakota?

The only reliable way to pin down your number is to pull General Price Lists from two or three Sioux Falls-area providers and compare line by line.

How much cheaper is cremation than burial in South Dakota?

Direct cremation in South Dakota starts around $1,500; a traditional funeral with burial averages $6,400, not counting the cemetery plot or vault. That is a difference of at least $4,900 for most families.

What rights do South Dakota families have when arranging a funeral?

Under the FTC Funeral Rule (16 CFR 453), every South Dakota funeral home must give you an itemized General Price List, quote prices by phone, and accept a casket or urn you bought elsewhere without a handling fee. South Dakota is not among the nine states the Funeral Consumers Alliance lists as requiring families to hire a funeral director. Complaints about a South Dakota funeral home go to the South Dakota State Board of Funeral Service (605-642-1600).

How can I save on funeral costs in South Dakota?

Start by lining up General Price Lists from two or three Sioux Falls-area providers before you commit to anything. Township and county cemeteries in South Dakota rarely appear in online searches but are often half the price of private cemeteries — call the clerk's office in the county where burial will occur. Independent, family-owned funeral homes still dominate South Dakota and tend to price more transparently than chain providers. Direct cremation at $1,500 remains the lowest-cost disposition in every South Dakota market.

Do I have to embalm a loved one in South Dakota?

The FTC Funeral Rule bars a South Dakota funeral home from saying embalming is required by law when it is not, and its price list must state that embalming is not required except in certain special cases. If a South Dakota provider says embalming is needed, ask which law or cemetery rule it is citing.

Is South Dakota cheaper or more expensive than the US average for funerals?

Compare the South Dakota figures in the cost table on this page with the national median of $8,300 for a funeral with viewing and burial, or $6,280 with cremation (NFDA 2023). Prices inside South Dakota vary by provider, so the General Price Lists you collect matter more than any average.

What is the cheapest funeral option in South Dakota?

Direct cremation is the lowest-cost option in South Dakota, starting at roughly $1,500. Direct burial comes next at around $3,200 and still avoids viewing, ceremony, and embalming.

What is the best way to find a cheap funeral home in South Dakota?

Call several South Dakota funeral homes and ask for prices by phone, which the FTC Funeral Rule requires them to give. Buying the casket or urn elsewhere is allowed under the FTC Funeral Rule, and the South Dakota home cannot charge a fee for it.
