    Request a Funeral Home General Price List: Letter and Phone Script

    By Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor · November 19, 2026

    The fastest way to know what a funeral will cost in your town is still the funeral home's own General Price List. This page gives you a short letter and a phone script so you can ask for that list today. It is educational, not legal advice.

    Print the letter, copy it into an email, or read the script. Then compare at least two or three lists side by side with our comparison worksheet. If you want a state baseline first, use funeral costs near me.

    Email or letter you can copy

    Subject: Request for your General Price List [Date] [Funeral home name] [City, state] Hello, I am comparing funeral options for my family and would like a copy of your current General Price List, including the casket price list and the outer burial container price list if you offer those items. I am requesting these lists under the FTC Funeral Rule (16 CFR Part 453). Please email them if you are able to. I would also like the current price for direct cremation and, if you offer it, immediate burial. Thank you, [Your name] [Phone or email]

    Phone script

    1. Ask: "I would like your current General Price List, please, including the casket and outer burial container lists if you have them."
    2. If they ask why: "I am comparing options for my family and want the itemized prices before we make decisions."
    3. Ask two follow-ups: "What is your direct cremation price, and does that include the crematory fee?" and "Can you email the lists today?"
    4. Write down the date, the person's name, and any prices quoted on the phone, then confirm them on the written list.

    Our questions to ask a funeral home list covers the rest of the conversation once you have the list in hand.

    Sources

    • Federal Trade Commission, Funeral Industry Practices (Funeral Rule), 16 CFR Part 453, including the General Price List and telephone-disclosure provisions.
    • FTC consumer information on the Funeral Rule, used here only for the public rights already stated in the rule itself.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does a funeral home have to give me a General Price List?

    A funeral home must answer price questions over the phone, and must give you a General Price List to keep when you meet in person to discuss arrangements. Many homes will also email the list if you ask. The rule is the FTC Funeral Rule, 16 CFR Part 453. This letter is a way to ask; it is not legal advice.

    What should I compare once I have two or three lists?

    Start with the basic services fee, transfer of remains, the direct cremation or immediate burial price, and any casket or urn prices you might actually buy. Then look at facilities, vehicles, and cash-advance items such as death certificates or obituaries. Our comparison worksheet is set up for that side-by-side review.

    Can I buy a casket or urn somewhere else?

    Yes. The Funeral Rule requires a funeral home to accept a casket or urn you buy from another seller, and it may not charge a handling fee for doing so. Confirm delivery timing with both the seller and the funeral home before you order.

    Is this letter a required government form?

    No. There is no official federal form for this request. The letter and script are a plain-language way to ask for the list the rule already requires the home to provide. You can edit the blanks or just use the phone script.
