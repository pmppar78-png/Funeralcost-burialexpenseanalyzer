    Real Funeral Home Prices in Austin

    By Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor · November 19, 2026

    Austin had more current, publicly posted General Price Lists than some larger metros we also checked, so this page collects four Austin firms whose own lists we could read on November 19, 2026.

    The sample is small on purpose. It does not cover every home in Travis County, and a printed line is not a binding quote. Ask for the list that is in force when you call, using our request letter and phone script.

    After.com TX3 is a direct-cremation office on N Lamar whose list says embalming and ID viewings are not offered. All Faiths posts one list that also covers a South Congress chapel; the row below uses the North IH-35 address. Affordable Burial & Cremation Service still posts a November 10, 2024 list; Weed Corley Fish's South Congress Dignity list is dated October 6, 2026.

    How to read these figures

    • After Cremation TX3. Figures are from the Austin licensee's General Price List PDF effective July 6, 2026. Time-of-need direct cremation is $995 with a family-provided or alternative container, and $1,245 with a cremation airtray. As on After.com's other Texas lists, the PDF states that embalming, ID viewings, and witness cremations are not offered, so those cells are recorded that way. Immediate burial is not itemized.
    • All Faiths Funeral Services. The same General Price List, effective November 12, 2026 on the cover and November 11, 2026 on later pages, is posted for both the North Austin chapel at 8507 N. IH-35 and the South Austin chapel at 4360 South Congress Ave., Ste. 115. This row uses the North address. Direct cremation with a container provided by the purchaser, or with a casket selected from the home (casket extra), is $1,945; with the home's alternative container is $2,095. Immediate burial with a casket provided by the purchaser, or selected from the home (casket extra), is $4,000; with the minimum casket is $5,095.
    • Affordable Burial & Cremation Service. This is the list the home still posts, effective November 10, 2024, under the Affordable Burial & Cremation Service name at the Dessau Road, Austin address (TFSC license 4121). Direct cremation with a container provided by the purchaser is $650; fiberboard containers from the home are listed at $699, $775, and $850. Immediate burial with a casket provided by the purchaser is $1,750; with the minimum Harmony casket is $2,300.
    • Weed Corley Fish Funeral Homes (South Congress). Official Dignity Memorial location price list for the South Congress Avenue, Austin chapel, effective October 6, 2026. Direct cremation with a container provided by the purchaser, or with a casket from the facility (casket extra), is $3,660; with the Safeway cremation container is $3,855. Immediate burial with a container provided by the purchaser is $4,695. Other Weed Corley Fish chapels have their own lists; figures here are only from the Austin South Congress PDF.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Are these Austin prices still good next week?

    Not necessarily. They are the figures on each posted General Price List as of the effective date shown, read on November 19, 2026. One list in this sample is still dated 2024 because that is what the home currently posts. Confirm the current list when you call.

    Why is All Faiths listed at only one Austin address?

    The November 2026 General Price List is shared by the North Austin chapel on IH-35 and the South Austin chapel on South Congress. We recorded one row, using the North address, rather than repeating the same prices twice.

    Do you list every Austin funeral home?

    No. This is four homes that posted a readable list. For more names, a directory such as Parting.com covers a wider set of providers. For a decision, compare current General Price Lists you request yourself.
