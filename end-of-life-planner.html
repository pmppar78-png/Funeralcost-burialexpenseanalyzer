    General Price List request letter

    Fill in the blanks, then print, email, or read this as a phone script. A funeral home must answer price questions over the phone, and must give you a General Price List to keep when you meet in person to discuss arrangements. Many homes will also email the list if you ask. You can also use our request letter and phone script page.

    Hello,

    I am requesting a current copy of your General Price List, and the casket and outer burial container price lists if you have them. I am requesting these lists under the FTC Funeral Rule (16 CFR Part 453). Please email them if you are able to. I would also like the current price for direct cremation and, if you offer it, immediate burial.

    Thank you,

    If you call, you may ask: "What is your direct cremation price, and does that include the crematory fee?" and "Can you email the lists today?" Write down the date, the person's name, and any prices quoted, then confirm them on the written list.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do you save what I type in this planner?

    No. This page does not submit the form to our servers, and we do not see or store what you type in this planner. If you want a copy, download the fillable PDF, use your browser's print or save function, or write on a printed page and keep it yourself.

    What should I leave off this form?

    Do not write passwords, Social Security numbers, PINs, or full account numbers. The documents section is only for where papers are kept, such as a drawer, a folder, or an attorney's office.

    Is this planner a will or a medical directive?

    No. It is a practical worksheet for funeral wishes, contacts, document locations, a budget, and a General Price List request. It is not a will, a trust, or an advance directive, and it is not legal advice.
