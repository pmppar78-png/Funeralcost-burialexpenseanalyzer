    Real Funeral Home Prices in New York City

    By Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor · November 19, 2026

    This page records General Price List figures that six New York City funeral homes posted on their own websites. We read those lists on November 19, 2026 and copied only the lines they printed.

    The sample is small on purpose. It is not a directory of every home in the five boroughs, and it is not a substitute for a current list from the home you are actually calling. Prices change. Use our request letter and phone script to ask for the list that is in force today.

    Four of the six lists are official Dignity Memorial location pages. Direct cremation at Plaza Jewish Community Chapel and at Walter B. Cooke is printed without the crematory charge; the other lists say when a crematory fee is included. Where a home lists facilities and supervision for visitation as two lines, this table keeps them separate rather than adding them together.

    How to read these figures

    • Plaza Jewish Community Chapel. Direct cremation prices do not include the crematory charge. Viewing figure is the published combined line for use of facilities and supervision for visitation.
    • Riverdale Funeral Home. Direct cremation and immediate burial are single published package prices. Facilities and supervision for visitation are listed separately; they are not added together here. A crematory fee of $552 is also listed as its own line.
    • Walter B. Cooke Funeral Home. Official Dignity Memorial location price list. Direct cremation prices do not include the crematory charge. Viewing figure is the published combined line for standard venue visitation plus supervision of visitation.
    • Riverside Memorial Chapel. Official Dignity Memorial location price list. Viewing figure is the published combined line for standard venue visitation plus supervision of visitation.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Are these quotes I can rely on for a funeral tomorrow?

    No. They are the figures printed on each home's posted General Price List on the effective date shown, as we read them on November 19, 2026. Ask the home for the list that is current when you call.

    Why do some New York City direct-cremation prices exclude the crematory fee?

    That is how those lists are written. Plaza Jewish Community Chapel and Walter B. Cooke state that the direct-cremation price does not include the crematory charge. Other homes on this page include it or list it as its own line. Read the source list before you compare two numbers.

    Do you have prices for every funeral home in New York City?

    No. This is a sample of six homes that posted a list we could read. For a wider local search, a directory such as Parting.com still covers more homes. For a decision, request current General Price Lists yourself.
