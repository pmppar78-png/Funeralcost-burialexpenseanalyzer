Burial Options in Colorado Springs

  • Traditional burial — Full funeral service with viewing, casket, and cemetery burial. The most traditional option with the highest cost.
  • Direct burial — Body is buried shortly after death without embalming, viewing, or ceremony. Saves significantly on facility and preparation costs.
  • Green burial — No embalming, biodegradable container, and a natural setting. Check for availability in the Colorado Springs area. Learn about green burial
  • Mausoleum entombment — Above-ground placement. Typically more expensive than ground burial but eliminates vault requirements.

Ways to Reduce Burial Costs in Colorado Springs

  • Compare prices from at least 3 funeral homes and cemeteries in the Colorado Springs area
  • Purchase caskets from independent retailers or online — savings of 50-70% are common. See our casket buying guide
  • Consider municipal or religious cemeteries, which often charge less than private ones
  • Ask about grave liner options, which cost less than full burial vaults
  • Explore burial insurance or payment assistance programs
  • Consider direct burial to eliminate embalming and facility costs
Save on Caskets: Under the FTC Funeral Rule, funeral homes must accept caskets purchased from third-party retailers without charging handling fees. Compare online casket retailers to save significantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does burial cost in Colorado Springs?

A traditional burial in Colorado Springs costs approximately $8,400 for the funeral service plus $3,990 for a cemetery plot. Including vault ($1,596), opening/closing fees ($1,995), casket ($2,520), and headstone ($1,500-$3,000), the total ranges from $15,981 to $22,491.

Is a burial vault required in Colorado Springs?

Burial vaults are required by most cemeteries in the Colorado Springs area as a matter of cemetery policy to prevent ground settling. They are rarely required by Colorado state law. A grave liner — a less expensive alternative — may meet the cemetery's requirements. Always ask your specific cemetery about their vault policy and whether cheaper alternatives are accepted.

What are the cheapest burial options in Colorado Springs?

The most affordable burial options in Colorado Springs include: direct burial (no viewing or ceremony), which can save thousands on embalming and facility fees; green burial using a biodegradable container and no embalming; and purchasing a casket from an independent retailer to save 50-70%. Municipal and religious cemeteries in Colorado Springs often have lower plot costs than private cemeteries.

How do Colorado Springs burial costs compare to Colorado?

Burial costs in Colorado Springs are close to the Colorado state average. At $8,400 for a funeral service (vs. $8,000 statewide) and $3,990 for a cemetery plot (vs. $3,800), the Colorado Springs metro premium reflects higher real estate and operating costs.
