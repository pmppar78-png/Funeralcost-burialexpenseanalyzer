Funeral Costs in Seattle, Washington (2026)

By: Paul Paradis, Editor Updated: April 2026 State: Washington

Funeral pricing in Seattle lands noticeably above the rest of Washington, with traditional services averaging around $11,000 and direct cremation starting near $2,625. The gap between providers in a single metro is often wider than the gap between cities — which is why comparison shopping matters most here.

Understanding Seattle Funeral Pricing

Funeral costs in Seattle carry a metro premium of approximately 25% above the Washington state average. This is driven by higher real estate costs for funeral home facilities, higher labor costs, and greater demand for services in a dense metropolitan area. However, the competitive market in Seattle also means you have more providers to choose from, which gives you leverage when comparing prices.

The most effective way to find the best price is to request General Price Lists from 2 to 3 funeral homes in the Seattle area and compare them line by line. Under the FTC Funeral Rule, every provider must give you this list upon request — no appointment needed, no commitment required.

Your Rights in Seattle

FTC Funeral Rule Protection Every funeral home in Seattle must provide an itemized General Price List, let you choose only the services you want, and accept caskets purchased elsewhere. You are never required to buy a package. Read the full FTC Funeral Rule guide.

Regional Context: Funeral Pricing in the West

Alternative dispositions — green burial, aquamation where legal, direct cremation, body donation — are more mainstream in Washington than in most of the country. If cost is the primary driver for your family, Washington's flexibility means you have options below the $8,800 traditional funeral price point that simply do not exist in the same way elsewhere. Charges to push back on

Many families in Seattle encounter unexpected charges. Watch for these common hidden fees:

  • Casket handling fees — Illegal under the FTC Funeral Rule, but some providers still add them
  • Mandatory embalming claims — Almost never legally required. Know your consumer rights
  • Package markups — Bundles often include unnecessary services. Compare line items. What funeral homes don't tell you
  • Weekend/holiday surcharges — Can add 15–25% to the total cost
  • Documentation fees — Death certificates and permits can add $200–$600

Questions Worth Asking First

What are funeral costs in the Seattle area?

Seattle funeral pricing centers on a $11,000 traditional-funeral average — higher than the Washington statewide $8,800. Direct cremation in Seattle starts around $2,625, and a cemetery plot runs about $5,250 on average. Expect totals from $2,625 at the low end to $19,550 for a full burial with all line items included.

Is cremation or burial more common in Seattle?

In the Seattle area, the cremation rate follows Washington's overall rate of 76%. Cremation remains the more affordable option, with direct cremation costing $2,625 compared to traditional burial at $11,000 plus cemetery fees of $5,250. Many Seattle families choose cremation to reduce costs while still holding a meaningful memorial service.

Is Seattle more or less expensive than the US average for funerals?

Seattle sits above the national $7,848 average for a traditional funeral — the cost of living in Seattle is a significant factor. Comparing providers in Seattle aggressively, or choosing direct cremation, is the reliable way to push costs down.

What is direct cremation in Seattle and how much does it cost?

Direct cremation in Seattle costs approximately $2,625 and is the most affordable option. It includes transportation to the crematory and return of ashes without a viewing or ceremony. Families can hold a separate memorial service at a later date, often at a fraction of the cost of a traditional funeral.

What are the cheapest funeral choices in Seattle?

Seattle has several affordable paths. Direct cremation at $2,625 is the cheapest disposition. Direct burial (no viewing or ceremony) is the cheapest burial. Memorial-only services at a venue you choose sidestep funeral-home facility fees. Independently purchased caskets save 50-70%. And veteran benefits, Medicaid assistance, and crowdfunding can close remaining gaps.
