Burial Costs in Mississippi (2026)

By: Paul Paradis, Editor Last updated: October 22, 2026 Region: South

Burial in Mississippi is almost never a single transaction. You pay the funeral home, you pay the cemetery, you pay the monument company, and frequently you pay a handful of administrative fees on top. Across those bills, Mississippi families typically see totals from $10,840 to $15,210 — with the ceiling often set by the casket and the cemetery choice rather than the ceremony itself.

How to Reduce Burial Costs in Mississippi

Burial costs in Mississippi can be managed with smart planning:

  • Request itemized pricing from every provider: Funeral homes in Mississippi must provide a General Price List. Cemeteries are not required to, but most in the Mississippi area will share one if you ask specifically for the itemized price sheet rather than a "package."
  • Check church and family cemeteries: Active church and family-owned cemeteries are still common in rural Mississippi and routinely price plots 30-50% below commercial memorial parks.
  • County veterans' sections: Many Mississippi counties maintain dedicated veterans' sections separate from national cemeteries.

Regional Context for Burial in Mississippi

In much of Mississippi, family and church cemeteries still operate alongside commercial ones, and many congregations keep section pricing below market. If you have a church or family plot in Mississippi, the difference from a private cemetery may be more than $1,040 (our estimate).

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does burial cost in Mississippi?

A traditional burial in Mississippi costs approximately $5,900 for the funeral service plus $2,600 for a cemetery plot. When you add a burial vault ($1,040), opening and closing fees ($1,300), casket ($1,770), and headstone ($1,000-$3,000), the total typically ranges from $10,840 to $15,210.

Are burial vaults mandatory in Mississippi?

A liner is usually an acceptable substitute — always ask the specific Mississippi cemetery for their written policy.

How can I reduce burial costs in Mississippi?

Direct (immediate) burial skips embalming, viewing and a ceremony at the funeral home, so it is usually the lowest-cost burial on a Mississippi price list. You may supply your own casket in Mississippi; the FTC Funeral Rule forbids a handling fee for it. Eligible veterans in Mississippi can be buried in a VA national cemetery at no cost for the grave, opening and closing, liner and marker.

How do I compare cemetery costs in Mississippi?

Cemetery costs in Mississippi vary significantly even within the same city.

What is an all-in burial estimate for Mississippi?

Adding every line item, burial in Mississippi usually totals $10,840 to $15,210: funeral service about $5,900, plot about $2,600, casket about $1,770, vault about $1,040, opening-and-closing about $1,300, and headstone $1,000-$3,000.

Can I choose a natural or green burial in Mississippi?

A growing number of Mississippi cemeteries now offer green burial sections (biodegradable container, no embalming, no vault), and the total cost is usually below a traditional burial. If a dedicated green burial ground is not nearby, some families in Mississippi use a hybrid approach (traditional plot without embalming).

Burial rules and help in Mississippi

For a license check or complaint about a funeral home arranging a burial in Mississippi, call the Mississippi State Board of Funeral Service at 601-932-1973; Mississippi cemeteries can have a separate regulator.

Mississippi is not on the Funeral Consumers Alliance list of nine states that restrict families from caring for their own dead. You can buy only the services you need, but someone must still file the Mississippi death certificate and get the permit.

A Mississippi burial needs a disposition permit. If you switch to cremation instead, there is no fixed waiting period, though a permit is required (15 Miss. Admin. Code Pt. 5, R. 4.7.2).

No verified statewide burial benefit is listed here for Mississippi. The county social-services or human-services office in Mississippi is the place to ask, and our assistance guide explains the federal options. Separately, Social Security pays Mississippi survivors $255 once, if they are an eligible spouse or child.

For Mississippi veterans, the plot and marker in a national cemetery are free, which can remove thousands from a burial bill. In Mississippi, VA national cemeteries accepting burials: Biloxi National Cemetery (228-388-6668); Corinth National Cemetery (901-386-8311); Natchez National Cemetery (601-445-4981); state veterans cemeteries: Mississippi Veterans Memorial Cemetery - Kilmichael (662-262-4232); Mississippi Veterans Memorial Cemetery - Newton (601-683-2959). Source: VA National Cemetery Administration.

What to do next

  1. Compare at least two Mississippi providers line by line, using their printed price lists.
  2. Check whether the Mississippi cemetery requires an outer burial container before you shop for one.
  3. Ask your Mississippi county human-services office about indigent burial help before you commit.
  4. If the person served, ask Biloxi National Cemetery what paperwork it needs, usually starting with the DD214 discharge record.
  5. Keep the board's number, 601-932-1973, in case a provider will not honor its price list.
