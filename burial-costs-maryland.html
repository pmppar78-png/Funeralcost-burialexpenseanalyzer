Burial Costs in Maryland (2026)

Reviewed: February 2026 Region: Northeast

Maryland's burial market sits at the crossroads of Southern tradition and mid-Atlantic urban density. The Washington, D.C. suburbs of Montgomery and Prince George's counties drive costs well above the state average, while the Eastern Shore, western Maryland mountains, and Baltimore's diverse neighborhoods each present their own character and pricing. The state's proximity to the nation's capital means a heavy military and veteran presence, with Baltimore National Cemetery — one of the original national cemeteries — and several other facilities serving those who served. Maryland's rich multicultural history, from its Catholic founding to its African-American heritage, creates a diverse burial landscape that spans from colonial-era churchyards to modern memorial parks.

What Drives Burial Costs in Maryland

D.C. Suburb Premium

Montgomery County and Prince George's County — the D.C. suburbs — have burial costs comparable to the most expensive metro areas in the nation. Plot prices of $4,000 to $10,000 are common. The density of population and high land values drive these costs.

Plot Prices by Area

D.C. suburbs: $4,000-$10,000. Baltimore: $2,500-$6,000. Annapolis area: $2,500-$5,500. Frederick/Hagerstown: $1,500-$3,500. Eastern Shore: $1,000-$3,000.

Notable Maryland Cemeteries

Baltimore National Cemetery, established in 1862, is one of the original national cemeteries. Loudon Park Cemetery in Baltimore is the largest cemetery in the state. Maryland Veterans Cemetery locations in Crownsville, Cheltenham, Flintstone, and Hurlock serve eligible veterans at no cost.

Cemetery Types Available in Maryland

  • Private memorial parks — Dominant in the D.C. suburbs and Baltimore metro.
  • Catholic cemeteries — Archdiocese of Baltimore and Archdiocese of Washington operate cemeteries in Maryland.
  • Veterans cemeteries — Baltimore National Cemetery and four state veterans cemeteries. Learn about veteran burial benefits
  • Municipal cemeteries — Available in many Maryland communities.
  • Historic churchyard cemeteries — Colonial-era burial grounds throughout the state.

Casket and Vault Options in Maryland

Maryland's competitive market offers many options. Under the FTC Funeral Rule, buy caskets from any retailer and save 50-80%.

Consumer Rights for Burial in Maryland

  • FTC Funeral Rule — Itemized pricing required.
  • Maryland State Board of Morticians and Funeral Directors — Regulates funeral homes.
  • Embalming not required by Maryland law.
  • Maryland has specific cemetery consumer protection laws covering perpetual care funds and sales practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does burial cost in Maryland?

A traditional burial costs approximately $9,200 for the funeral service plus $4,600 for a plot. Total costs range from $17,940 to $25,300. D.C. suburbs are the most expensive.

Is a burial vault required in Maryland?

No state law requires vaults. Most cemeteries require them. Green burial sections may waive this requirement.

What are the cheapest burial options in Maryland?

Direct burial on the Eastern Shore or western Maryland costs $8,000-$12,000. Online caskets save $1,500-$5,000. Four state veterans cemeteries provide free burial for eligible veterans.

How many veterans cemeteries does Maryland have?

Maryland has Baltimore National Cemetery plus four state veterans cemeteries in Crownsville, Cheltenham, Flintstone, and Hurlock, providing broad geographic coverage for the state's veteran population. Learn about veteran benefits
