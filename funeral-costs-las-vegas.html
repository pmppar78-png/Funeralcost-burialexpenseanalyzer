Funeral Costs in Las Vegas, Nevada (2026)

By: Paul Paradis, Editor Updated: April 2026 State: Nevada

Clark County's funeral market is shaped by two things most metros don't share: a transient population that often needs remains shipped elsewhere, and a 72 percent cremation rate that sits among the highest in the nation. Major providers include Palm Mortuary (a Dignity chain with six Las Vegas valley locations including Palm Eastern, Palm Northwest, and Palm Downtown), Davis Funeral Homes & Memorial Park in the northwest valley, Bunker Family Mortuary in North Las Vegas, and Kraft-Sussman Funeral Services on Sahara. Low-cost specialists Affordable Cremation & Burial Services, Neptune Society of Northern Nevada (with Las Vegas service), and Simple Cremation Las Vegas publish direct cremation rates that undercut the $1,980 metro average by $500-$900. Palm Eastern Mortuary & Cemetery on East Sunrise, Bunkers Memory Gardens, and Woodlawn Cemetery carry most of the burial volume, with Southern Nevada Veterans Memorial Cemetery in Boulder City serving the VA-eligible.

What Each Line Item Costs for Las Vegas

These figures draw on 2025-2026 General Price Lists from Clark County funeral homes, Nevada State Board of Funeral and Cemetery Services licensing data, and crematory capacity surveys for the southern Nevada region, cross-checked against the NFDA 2024 GPL Study. Each GPL was obtained under the FTC Funeral Rule (16 CFR Part 453). Established burial grounds such as Woodlawn Cemetery near downtown and Palm Memorial Park in Henderson anchor local plot pricing. The Las Vegas market's 10 percent premium over the Nevada average tracks facility overhead (valley land is not cheap) and the labor cost of operating 24/7 shipping desks for out-of-state repatriation — a necessity in a metro where the majority of deceased residents were born somewhere else.

Out-of-State Shipment: The Line Item No Other Metro Calculates

Roughly 60 percent of Clark County residents were born outside Nevada, and a meaningful share of families still want burial in a home state — California, Utah, Illinois, New York, the Philippines. Out-of-state shipment of remains by air from McCarran (now Harry Reid International) runs $1,500-$3,200 depending on destination, with a second funeral home's receiving fee at the other end adding $800-$1,400. International repatriation to Mexico or the Philippines often reaches $4,500-$7,500 all-in. Most Las Vegas mortuaries have in-house shipping coordinators, but the pricing is rarely on the GPL — you have to ask. For families wanting a burial at Rose Hills in Whittier or a Catholic cemetery in Los Angeles County, air freight through Palm Mortuary or Davis Funeral Homes is usually handled start-to-finish.

Nevada's 72 Percent Cremation Rate and What It Does to Pricing

Nevada has one of the highest cremation rates in the United States, driven by a combination of relocated retirees, multi-state families scattering ashes, and a dry climate that simply does not carry the burial tradition of the Midwest or South. The practical effect on Las Vegas funeral pricing: direct-cremation specialists compete hard, with rates starting near $895-$1,200 at Neptune Society, Affordable Cremation & Burial, and Simple Cremation Las Vegas. Full-service traditional funeral homes make up for thinner traditional volume by bundling cremation-with-service packages in the $5,500-$7,200 range. Ask any provider for both: a basic direct cremation number and a cremation-with-memorial number. The difference reveals their markup pattern.

Line Items Las Vegas Families Miss on the First Read

  • Basic services of funeral director and staff — non-declinable. Ranges from $1,900 at smaller Henderson chapels to $3,400 at Palm valley locations.
  • Refrigeration — Nevada's dry heat makes refrigeration effectively mandatory. Charged $75-$120 per day after the first 24 hours at most providers.
  • Transportation within the valley — $395-$625 from a Summerlin or Henderson home or hospital to a downtown chapel.
  • Shipping container for air freight — a required combo container (different from a casket) runs $400-$800 for domestic shipping, more for international.
  • Clark County death certificates — $25 each through Clark County Office of Vital Records, up from $20 a few years ago.
  • Cemetery endowment care fees — separate from the plot itself at Palm Eastern, Davis, and Woodlawn; often 10-15 percent of plot price.

Southern Nevada Veterans Memorial Cemetery and Clark County Assistance

The Southern Nevada Veterans Memorial Cemetery in Boulder City — about 25 minutes from downtown Las Vegas — provides burial at no cost for eligible veterans and their spouses, including opening and closing, the concrete liner, and a government-issued headstone. The VA national burial allowance (up to $2,000 service-connected, $900 non-service-connected) can offset funeral costs at any provider. Clark County's Social Services Department administers an indigent burial/cremation program for qualifying residents who die without resources and without family able to pay, typically covering direct cremation and a modest committal. Nevada's Victims of Crime Program through the state Attorney General's office can cover up to $7,500 in funeral expenses when death results from a criminal act. HELP of Southern Nevada and Catholic Charities of Southern Nevada both maintain limited emergency funeral funds. The Funeral Consumers Alliance of Nevada maintains Las Vegas price surveys that consumers can reference when requesting GPLs.

Questions From Families

What are funeral costs in the Las Vegas area?

The Las Vegas valley averages $8,800 for a full traditional service, $6,820 for a cremation-with-service package, $1,980 for direct cremation, and $4,180 for a cemetery plot. Realistic totals span $895 (Neptune Society or Simple Cremation Las Vegas direct cremation) through roughly $15,600 for a full traditional burial at Palm Eastern or Davis Funeral Homes with a valley plot and monument. Out-of-state shipment adds $1,500-$3,200.

Do more Las Vegas families choose cremation or burial?

Clark County's cremation rate sits near 72 percent, among the highest in the nation and ahead of Nevada's statewide figure. Three forces drive it: a transient population whose families are scattered across multiple states, a high share of residents who explicitly prefer cremation for cost and logistics, and aggressive direct-cremation pricing from Neptune Society, Affordable Cremation & Burial, and Simple Cremation Las Vegas. Memorial services after cremation — often held at casino resort banquet rooms, family homes, or Red Rock visitor areas — are common.

How do Las Vegas funeral costs compare to the national average?

Las Vegas runs above the national traditional-funeral average of $7,848. Southern Nevada real estate, refrigeration costs in a desert climate, and the operational cost of running out-of-state shipping desks add to the base. It is more expensive than Phoenix or Tucson, comparable to Denver, and meaningfully cheaper than Los Angeles or the Bay Area. The spread between a Palm chapel and a direct-cremation specialist is often $5,000-$7,000 on comparable dispositions.

Are there affordable funeral options in Las Vegas?

The lowest-cost option is direct cremation at $895-$1,200 through Neptune Society or Simple Cremation Las Vegas. Other paths: direct burial without visitation at a valley cemetery, a memorial-only gathering held outside a funeral home, veteran burial at Southern Nevada Veterans Memorial Cemetery in Boulder City at no cost, or Clark County Social Services indigent disposition assistance for qualifying residents. Nevada Victims of Crime Program covers up to $7,500 if death resulted from a criminal act.
