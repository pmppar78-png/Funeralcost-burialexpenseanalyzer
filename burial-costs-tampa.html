Burial Costs in Tampa, Florida (2026)

By: Paul Paradis, Editor Updated: April 2026 State: Florida

Burial in Tampa sits at the intersection of two Florida cemetery traditions: the Victorian garden cemetery preserved at Oaklawn downtown and the palm-shaded corporate memorial parks that spread across Hillsborough and Pinellas counties through the 20th century. Myrtle Hill Memorial Park on South 22nd Street, one of the largest active cemeteries in the metro, processes a significant share of Tampa Bay's annual burials. Total costs here range from $16,104 to $22,638, a bit above the Florida statewide average.

A Tampa traditional burial commonly totals $16,104 to $22,638. The spread reflects whether a family uses a corporate park like Myrtle Hill or a Catholic cemetery such as Calvary on West Columbus Drive, and whether the casket comes from the funeral home or an independent retailer.

Florida's high water table and what it does to a burial invoice

Coastal Florida cemeteries deal with water-table realities that northern cemeteries do not. Many Tampa Bay burial grounds require reinforced concrete vaults or, increasingly, above-ground entombment in a community mausoleum. In some sections of lower-elevation cemeteries, above-ground is actually cheaper than in-ground burial plus a premium vault, because the vault requirement drops away. Always price both options at the same cemetery.

The Florida DFS preneed database: a shopping tool

Florida's Division of Funeral, Cemetery and Consumer Services — part of the Department of Financial Services — maintains a consumer complaint database that is actually useful. Before signing any preneed contract at a Tampa Bay provider, check it. Preneed arrangements are regulated stringently under Florida statute and must be backed by a licensed trust or insurance contract; the database shows which providers have drawn complaints over the prior years. The division also enforces the General Price List requirement: every licensed Tampa funeral home must provide one on first in-person inquiry. Under the FTC Funeral Rule, those price lists must be itemized — no bundled-only pricing.

A Tampa-specific shopping tactic

Walk into three Tampa Bay funeral homes on the same afternoon and request each one's General Price List and Casket Price List. You will legally leave with the documents. In Tampa, the largest variation between providers is not the professional-services fee — it is the casket markup. Local homes routinely add 300–400% over wholesale. Pairing a direct-burial package from the cheapest local mortuary with a casket from an online retailer typically knocks $2,000 off the midpoint. The casket buying guide and best online casket retailers show the price floor.

Hillsborough indigent burial and Cuban-community traditions

Hillsborough County maintains a limited indigent-burial benefit through its social services office for qualifying low-income residents; the allowance is modest and aimed at direct-burial cases. Separately, Tampa's Cuban, Haitian, and Caribbean communities maintain distinct burial traditions, and several funeral homes — notably in Ybor City and West Tampa — specialize in accommodating them with Catholic-cemetery coordination and family-centered viewings. For broader help, the funeral payment assistance guide covers Social Security lump-sum and FEMA reimbursement; cheapest funeral options layers in direct burial and green burial routes.

Negotiating above-ground: In Pinellas and low-lying Hillsborough sections, mausoleum entombment can come in cheaper than in-ground burial plus a premium vault. Request both quotes side by side; the delta can be several thousand dollars, and Florida law does not require a vault — only individual cemetery policy does. Monument work via a local stone company also routinely beats cemetery-vendor pricing 25–35% (headstone cost guide).

Common Questions We Hear

How much does burial cost in Tampa?

A traditional burial in Tampa costs approximately $8,580 for the funeral service plus $3,960 for a cemetery plot. Including vault ($1,584), opening/closing fees ($1,980), casket ($2,574), and headstone ($1,500-$3,000), the total ranges from $16,104 to $22,638.

Is a burial vault required in Tampa?

Florida law does not require a vault. Tampa Bay cemeteries routinely require either a vault or a concrete grave liner because of the high water table — especially in Pinellas and coastal Hillsborough. Myrtle Hill, Calvary, and Garden of Memories each publish their own vault policy. A liner commonly satisfies the requirement at roughly 60% of vault cost.

How do Tampa burial costs compare to Florida?

Tampa is about 10% above the Florida statewide average — $8,580 for service against $7,800, and $3,960 for a plot against $3,600. Tampa is below Miami and West Palm Beach, roughly equal to Orlando, and slightly above Jacksonville. The Division of Funeral, Cemetery and Consumer Services regulates pricing disclosure across all Florida providers.
