Affiliate Disclosure

By Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor · Last updated October 29, 2026

Funeral Cost & Burial Expense Analyzer is an independent, educational website. We are not a funeral home, cemetery, crematory, insurance company, or insurance agent, and we do not sell funeral goods or services. This page explains how the site is funded. The site is owned and edited by Paul Paradis; questions about this page can go to contact@funeralcostanalyzer.com.

Affiliate links

As of October 22, 2026, no link on this site is a paid affiliate link we placed by hand (see automatic link conversion below); every outbound link, including those to casket and urn sellers, is an ordinary link. If we join a program later, clicking such a link and then buying or signing up may earn us a commission from that company or from an affiliate network. This does not add to the price you pay. Any such link will be marked with rel="sponsored" in the page code, with a visible disclosure placed near it.

No company we mention is an affiliate partner today. If we join programs later, the likely candidates are online casket or urn retailers, estate-planning services and insurance comparison tools, and this page will name them.

Automatic affiliate links (Sovrn Commerce / VigLink)

As of October 29, 2026, this site uses Sovrn Commerce (formerly VigLink), a service that may automatically convert some ordinary links to outside stores and services into affiliate links. If you click one of these links and then buy or sign up, we may earn a commission. This never costs you anything extra, and it never affects our rankings or recommendations: which companies we cover, how we rank them, and what we say about them are decided before and regardless of any link conversion.

Advertising

This site shows display advertising served by third-party ad networks (for example, Google AdSense). Advertisers do not choose, review, or approve our content, and an ad appearing on a page is not an endorsement by us.

Editorial independence

  • Compensation does not determine which providers or products we cover or how we describe them.
  • We do not accept payment in exchange for favorable coverage.
  • Cost figures are estimates based on published sources and may not match prices in your area. Ask funeral homes for their itemized General Price List, which the FTC Funeral Rule requires them to provide.
  • How we research content is described in our Editorial Standards.

Not professional advice

Information on this site is general and educational. It is not financial, legal, tax, insurance, or funeral-director advice. See our full Disclaimer.

What kinds of links could earn a commission

As of October 2026 we have placed no paid links by hand on this site, but Sovrn Commerce (VigLink) may automatically convert some ordinary links into affiliate links that can earn a commission at no extra cost to you, and this never affects our rankings or recommendations. Funeral Cost Analyzer is a one-person educational site. If we add paid links later, the pages that could carry a commercial link are the ones that already discuss a product category: caskets and urns, final-expense insurance, prepaid funeral plans, grief counseling, and occasional estate-planning tools. A link is not a ranking. No company named on the site is a hand-picked partner; Sovrn Commerce is the only affiliate service in use. Every hand-placed tracking link is switched off, so each outbound link is written as an ordinary one and none is marked as sponsored, although Sovrn Commerce may convert some of them automatically. If that changes, the new partner will be named on this page before its link goes live.

How a name gets onto a page

A provider is mentioned because a family is likely to meet that name while shopping: a large retailer, a carrier that advertises burial insurance, a counseling service, a medical-school donation program. The test is whether a reader can act on the paragraph without the link. Prices, when we print them, come from the NFDA 2023 General Price List Study (latest available), the FTC Funeral Rule, a government page, or a figure already tied to a source on that guide. They do not come from an affiliate network's media kit.

We do not sell placements. A company cannot pay to be called "best," to move up a list, or to have a criticism removed. If a contract ever required that, we would not sign it. If commissions ever exist, they would be paid by the advertiser or the network after a purchase or a signup. They would not be added to the price you pay, and they would not be a reason to prefer one funeral home, cemetery, or policy over another.

Commissions do not set the order

Lists such as the casket retailers page and the burial-insurance page are ordered by the question the page is answering: price transparency, what the contract actually pays, financial-strength ratings the carrier already publishes, and whether a family can leave with the General Price List. If we add partners, a partner would not be moved above a company we cannot monetize. If that ever happens by mistake, the correction belongs in the paragraph, and you can write to us from the contact page.

How often this disclosure changes

This page is reviewed when a guide that contains commercial links is edited, and at least when the date under the heading changes. The review on 2026-10-22 checked that the disclosure still matches the way links are marked (rel="sponsored" would be used only if a tracking link were switched on, and none is) and that no guide promises a percent-off we cannot source. It confirmed there are still no partners. Editorial rules for the cost figures themselves are on the editorial standards page.

Advertising is separate. Display ads, including Google AdSense, are sold by the ad network. We do not pick the advertiser on a given page view, and an ad is not an endorsement. The privacy policy explains the cookies those ads use.
