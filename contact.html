Looking for local funeral prices? Here’s how to get them yourself

We do not collect reader information, match families to providers, or earn referral fees from funeral homes. We don’t keep a database of “partner” funeral homes and we won’t make calls on your behalf. Here’s how to find real, current local pricing on your own:

  • Request General Price Lists (GPLs): Under the FTC Funeral Rule, every funeral home must give you an itemized price list on request, in person or over the phone. Call 2–3 providers in your ZIP code and ask for theirs.
  • Use our comparison worksheet: The free comparison worksheet lines up GPL quotes side by side so the differences are easy to see.
  • Check FCA affiliates: The Funeral Consumers Alliance has local affiliates that track provider pricing in many metros and may publish comparison data for your area.
  • See state and city pages on this site: Our state and national cost index pages show typical ranges so you can recognize a high or low quote when you see one.

If you need time-sensitive help during an active funeral arrangement, please contact a licensed funeral director or your local FCA affiliate directly — we are an educational resource and cannot assist with immediate arrangements.
