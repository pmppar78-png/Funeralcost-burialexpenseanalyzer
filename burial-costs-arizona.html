Burial Costs in Arizona (2026)

By: Paul Paradis, Editor Last updated: October 22, 2026 Region: West

Burial in Arizona is almost never a single transaction. You pay the funeral home, you pay the cemetery, you pay the monument company, and frequently you pay a handful of administrative fees on top. Across those bills, Arizona families typically see totals from $13,660 to $19,220 — with the ceiling often set by the casket and the cemetery choice rather than the ceremony itself.
