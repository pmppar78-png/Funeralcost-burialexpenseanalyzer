Burial Costs in Delaware (2026)

Reviewed: February 2026 Region: Northeast

As the second-smallest state in the nation, Delaware's burial market is intimate but well-established, shaped by a blend of historic Quaker and colonial cemeteries, modern memorial parks, and a strong mix of denominational burial grounds. Wilmington and the northern New Castle County corridor — influenced by proximity to Philadelphia — carry higher costs, while the slower-paced communities of Kent and Sussex Counties on the Delmarva Peninsula offer more affordable options. Delaware's long history means that some of the oldest cemeteries on the Eastern Seaboard are found here, alongside newer facilities designed to serve the state's steadily growing population.

What Drives Burial Costs in Delaware

Cemetery Types Across the State

Delaware's cemetery landscape includes municipal cemeteries in Wilmington, Dover, and other cities; denominational cemeteries operated by Catholic, Methodist, and other religious groups; and private memorial parks serving the New Castle County market. Historic Quaker burial grounds, some dating to the 1600s, are found throughout the state. The Delaware Veterans Memorial Cemetery in Bear serves eligible veterans at no cost.

Plot Prices by Area

Wilmington/New Castle County: $2,500-$7,000 per plot. Dover/Kent County: $1,500-$4,000. Sussex County/beach communities: $1,500-$4,500. Rural areas of Kent and Sussex counties offer the most affordable plots at $1,000-$2,500.

Vault and Liner Requirements

Delaware has no state law requiring burial vaults. Most commercial cemeteries require a vault or grave liner, but some older church cemeteries and rural burial grounds may be more flexible. Always ask about grave liner alternatives.

Notable Delaware Cemeteries

The Delaware Veterans Memorial Cemetery in Bear provides free burial for eligible veterans. Wilmington and Brandywine Cemetery, founded in 1843, is one of the state's most historic and architecturally significant burial grounds. Old Swedes Burial Ground in Wilmington dates to the colonial Swedish settlement.

Cemetery Types Available in Delaware

  • Municipal cemeteries — Available in Wilmington, Dover, Newark, and other cities.
  • Denominational cemeteries — Catholic, Methodist, Presbyterian, and Quaker cemeteries throughout the state.
  • Private memorial parks — Commercial cemeteries primarily in New Castle County.
  • Veterans cemeteries — Delaware Veterans Memorial Cemetery in Bear. Learn about veteran burial benefits
  • Historic burial grounds — Colonial and early American cemeteries, some still accepting burials.

Casket and Vault Options in Delaware

Delaware's small market means fewer funeral homes, but families have full access to online casket retailers under the FTC Funeral Rule. Online caskets save 50-80% compared to funeral home prices. Delaware's location in the mid-Atlantic corridor means fast shipping from major distribution centers.

Consumer Rights for Burial in Delaware

  • FTC Funeral Rule — Itemized pricing required. Select only the services you want.
  • Delaware Board of Funeral Services — Licenses funeral homes and handles consumer complaints.
  • Embalming not required by Delaware law for prompt burial.
  • Prepaid funeral protections — Delaware requires prepaid funeral funds to be held in trust.

Green and Natural Burial in Delaware

Green burial options in Delaware are limited but growing. Some cemeteries accommodate natural burial requests, allowing biodegradable caskets and skipping embalming. Delaware's proximity to green burial pioneers in neighboring states provides additional options for families willing to travel. Green burial saves 30-50% compared to traditional burial. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does burial cost in Delaware?

A traditional burial in Delaware costs approximately $8,600 for the funeral service plus $4,200 for a cemetery plot. Total costs range from $16,220 to $22,830. New Castle County (Wilmington area) is the most expensive part of the state.

Is a burial vault required in Delaware?

Delaware has no state law requiring burial vaults. Most commercial cemeteries require them, but some church and historic cemeteries may be more flexible. Ask about a less expensive grave liner as an alternative.

What are the cheapest burial options in Delaware?

Direct burial at a municipal or church cemetery in Kent or Sussex County is most affordable, typically $7,000-$10,000 total. Buying a casket online saves $1,000-$4,000. Veterans qualify for free burial at the Delaware Veterans Memorial Cemetery in Bear.

Where is the Delaware Veterans Memorial Cemetery?

The Delaware Veterans Memorial Cemetery is located in Bear, Delaware (New Castle County). It provides free burial for eligible veterans and their spouses. Visit our veteran burial benefits guide for details.
