    Parting.com and Funeral Cost Analyzer: A Factual 2026 Comparison

    By Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor · November 19, 2026

    Parting.com is a consumer directory of funeral homes. A live check of parting.com on November 19, 2026 found a city or ZIP search, state and city funeral-home lists, and on-page filters for service type. That is a real local-price product. Funeral Cost Analyzer does not have an equivalent database of funeral-home General Price Lists, and this page will not pretend otherwise.

    What we built instead, for people who start with a state question or who want to collect those lists themselves, is a ZIP or state lookup of our published 2026 estimates, plus a letter and phone script to request a General Price List under the FTC Funeral Rule. We also link to Parting when a reader wants a directory search, and we say clearly that we do not operate that site.

    Where Parting.com still leads

    If your question is "what do funeral homes near this ZIP charge," Parting is built for that. We cannot honestly close that gap without collecting or licensing General Price Lists, and we have not done that as of November 19, 2026.

    Where this site adds something Parting does not try to be

    We publish a dated national index, state and burial and cremation guides, official-source citations (including NFDA's public 2023 medians), consumer-rights explainers, and calculators. Those are the jobs we can do without inventing a local price.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is Funeral Cost Analyzer a Parting.com alternative for local prices?

    Only in a limited sense. We can give you a state estimate and a way to request a General Price List. We cannot show you a table of funeral-home prices for your ZIP the way Parting does. If that local table is what you need, Parting is the better fit for that task.

    Do you earn money when I click through to Parting.com?

    Parting is not a paid placement or one of our listed affiliate programs. Like other outbound links here, a Parting link may be converted automatically by Sovrn Commerce (see our affiliate disclosure).

    Will you build a funeral-home price database later?

    Only if we can collect or license real General Price Lists and label them honestly. We will not invent city prices by multiplying a state average. Until that data exists here, this comparison will keep saying Parting leads on local prices.
