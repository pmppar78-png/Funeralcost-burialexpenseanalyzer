    Real Funeral Home Prices in Los Angeles

    By Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor · November 19, 2026

    This page collects General Price List figures from five Los Angeles funeral homes that published a list on their own sites. We recorded those numbers on November 19, 2026, with the effective date each list prints.

    Treat every line as a snapshot, not a binding quote. A home can change its list without updating a PDF the same day. Before you choose a provider, request its current General Price List with our letter and phone script.

    Angelus Funeral Home's direct-cremation figure is a package total before tax and includes a discounted basic services fee, which is why it can be lower than the standalone basic fee. Hollywood Forever prints a lower direct-cremation price with its own alternative container than with a container you supply; that inversion is as published. Chevra Kadisha Mortuary states that embalming and cremation are not available.

    How to read these figures

    • Angelus Funeral Home. Direct cremation is a published package total before tax and includes a discounted basic services fee of $1,337.50. Immediate burial is the no-ceremony price with the casket not included. Viewing is use of facility for viewing, per day.
    • House of Winston Funeral Services. Direct cremation is published as starting at $900. Embalming is listed as $550 up to 250 lbs. Viewing is the published viewing/visitations line.
    • Hollywood Forever (Hollywood Funeral Home). Basic services is printed with an asterisk on the list. Direct cremation with an alternative container from the home is $3,500; with a container provided by the purchaser is printed as $4,387. Direct burial with a casket from the home is $3,600; with a purchaser container is $3,737. Those inversions are as published.
    • Home of Peace Mortuary. Direct cremation is the published line with a container provided by the purchaser. Immediate burial with a purchaser casket is $3,195; with a casket from the home the published range is $4,090–$19,190. No standalone viewing line was printed.
    • Chevra Kadisha Mortuary. The list states that embalming and cremation are not available. Direct burial with a casket provided by the purchaser is $2,992.00; with a grey cloth wood-product casket is $3,538.98. The $395 figure is use of the mortuary chapel, not a separately labeled visitation.

    Each source is the mortuary's own list, marked rel=nofollow. None of them is an affiliate placement. For statewide planning estimates, see the California funeral cost guide.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why is one Los Angeles direct-cremation price a package total?

    Angelus Funeral Home prints a direct-cremation package total before tax, and that package includes a discounted basic services fee. The table shows the published total and explains the discount in the notes, rather than replacing the number with a reconstructed one.

    Is the House of Winston 'starting at $900' figure a complete direct cremation?

    It is the price the list prints for direct cremation, labeled 'starting at $900.' We recorded it as published and used $900 as the lowest labeled figure for the sample median. Confirm with the home what that starting price includes.

    Can I use these Los Angeles figures instead of calling a funeral home?

    No. They help you see what five posted lists said on the dates shown. The price that matters is the current General Price List from the home you will actually use.
