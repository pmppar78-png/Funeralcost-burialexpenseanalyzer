What Pushes a Delaware Funeral Bill Up or Down

A Delaware quote rests on the home's own costs, how many homes compete across New Castle, Kent and Sussex counties, and the steps state rules add, such as the chief medical examiner's cremation permit and the 24-hour rule for embalming, refrigeration or a sealed casket. Our $8,600 traditional estimate sits above the $8,300 US median, as Northeast prices usually do, and quotes between a Wilmington home and a Sussex County one can differ widely, so price more than one county.

At 55%, Delaware's cremation rate is 8.4 points below the 63.4% national rate that NFDA projected for 2025.

Rights Every Funeral Buyer Has in Delaware

Know Your Rights Under the FTC Funeral Rule Every funeral home in Delaware must comply with the Federal Trade Commission's Funeral Rule, which protects consumers nationwide.

If you believe a funeral home in Delaware has violated these rights, you can file a complaint with the FTC at ftc.gov or contact the Funeral Consumers Alliance for guidance.

Help Paying for a Funeral in Delaware

  • Delaware's $100 DSS payment is small, so ask the Division of Social Services about its separate indigent burial service as well when no one can pay.
  • Social Security's $255 is separate from Delaware's $100 DSS payment and goes only to a surviving spouse or eligible child, who must phone 1-800-772-1213 within two years of the death.
  • For a Delaware veteran, whoever paid the funeral home can file VA Form 21P-530EZ: up to $1,032 for burial and $1,032 for a plot when the death, on or after October 1, 2026, was not service-connected. A plot may not be needed at all if the veteran is buried at one of the two state veterans cemeteries; see our veteran burial benefits guide.
  • Beyond the DSS payment and indigent burial service, our funeral payment assistance guide covers Medicaid and charities, and how to pay for a funeral with no money sets out a plan when Delaware relatives have nothing to put toward the bill.

Steps for Planning a Funeral in Delaware

Families arranging a funeral in Delaware for the first time often move faster than they need to.

  1. Decide on disposition: cremation in Delaware or burial in Delaware.

Delaware death certificates cost $25 each

Every certified copy of a Delaware death certificate is $25, with no discount for extra copies, and part of each fee goes to the state's distressed cemetery fund. If no record is found the fee is kept for the search (Delaware Office of Vital Statistics, death certificate application; checked October 29, 2026).

  • Proof: send a copy of photo ID and, unless you are the surviving spouse, proof of relationship such as a birth certificate.
  • Offices: Dover (417 Federal Street, 302-744-4549), Newark and Georgetown. Credit-card orders go through GoCertificates or VitalChek for an extra fee.
  • Veterans: state law gives a veteran's relative one free certified copy when it is needed to settle the veteran's affairs (16 Del. C. § 3132(d)).

Deaths from 1985 and earlier are held by the Delaware Public Archives (302-744-5000), not the vital statistics office.

Delaware funeral oversight, DSS funeral help and veterans' cemeteries

Delaware funeral homes are licensed through the Delaware Division of Professional Regulation, which you can reach at 302-744-4500. Use that number if a home will not show its prices, piles on charges you did not ask for, or pushes you to commit on the first visit. The division can also confirm that a funeral director's license is active.

The Funeral Consumers Alliance does not include Delaware among the nine states that require a funeral director for after-death care, so the family may buy only the services it actually wants. Hiring a home is a choice for most steps, not a legal duty.

The medical examiner's permit

Delaware has no waiting period before cremation, but no cremation may go ahead without a permit signed by the chief medical examiner and an authorization from the next of kin (16 Del. C. § 3159). Ask the home how long the permit is taking, since that sets the earliest date for a memorial held after cremation.

What the Division of Social Services pays

Two forms of help exist. First, DSS pays up to $100 toward funeral expenses when the person was on TANF, General Assistance or Medicaid at death (16 Del. Admin. Code 2000-2020). The next of kin sends a written request with the death certificate and proof of the bill, and the money goes to the funeral home. Second, DSS runs an indigent burial service that buries indigent or unclaimed people at state expense. Neither comes near the $8,600 average Delaware funeral in the table, and Social Security's $255, paid only to a qualifying spouse or child, does not change that much.

Veterans

The state operates Delaware Veterans Memorial Cemetery in New Castle County (302-834-8046) and in Sussex County (302-934-5653). Both take burial requests directly, and the VA provides the headstone or marker at no charge. Ask the cemetery about any fee before paying for a private plot, which averages $4,200 in the table.

Steps for a Delaware family

  1. Collect written price lists from at least two homes before agreeing to anything.
  2. Decide early about embalming and a viewing; leaving them out is often the biggest saving.
  3. If the person was on TANF, General Assistance or Medicaid, send DSS the request, the death certificate and the bill.
  4. For a veteran, compare the New Castle County veterans cemetery with a private plot.
  5. Keep 302-744-4500 in case a home does not honor its price list.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average price of a funeral in Delaware right now?

We put a Delaware traditional funeral at about $8,600, a funeral with cremation at $6,800 and direct cremation from roughly $2,000. A cemetery plot adds about $4,200. These are our estimates, built from the NFDA national median, so a Wilmington, Dover or Sussex County home's General Price List is the figure to trust.

Is cremation cheaper than burial in Delaware?

Yes. Direct cremation near $2,000 costs about $6,600 less than an $8,600 traditional funeral, and more than $10,000 less once a $4,200 plot is counted. Delaware sets no waiting period, but the chief medical examiner must sign the cremation permit first (16 Del. C. § 3159).

What rights do Delaware families have when arranging a funeral?

Delaware families have the federal Funeral Rule protections: an itemized price list, prices by phone, the freedom to skip any service you do not want, and the right to use a casket or urn bought elsewhere. Delaware does not require a funeral director for every step. Complaints go to the Division of Professional Regulation at 302-744-4500.

How do I keep funeral costs down in Delaware?

Compare price lists from more than one county, skip embalming and a viewing if they are not important to you, and look at direct cremation from about $2,000. A veteran may be buried at the New Castle County or Sussex County veterans cemetery, and a person on TANF, General Assistance or Medicaid qualifies for the $100 DSS funeral payment.

Does Delaware require embalming?

Not if you choose refrigeration or a sealed casket. Under the Division of Public Health's rule (16 Del. Admin. Code 4602 § 10), a body kept more than 24 hours must be embalmed, placed in an industry-standard refrigerator, or put in a sealed casket that is not opened again, and burial or cremation has to happen within five days unless the state issues a permit.

Bodies stored in a receiving vault must be embalmed, and a body shipped by common carrier more than 24 hours after death must be embalmed or sealed in a metal case. Embalming in Delaware costs about $860 on this page's estimate.

Is Delaware cheaper or more expensive than the US average for funerals?

Delaware keeps funeral homes and cemeteries apart. Under 24 Del. C. §3119, a licensee may not interfere with a family's free choice of funeral home, run a mortuary inside a cemetery, or take any fee or benefit from a cemetery, crematory or natural organic reduction facility. Compare Delaware quotes with the national median of $8,300 with viewing and burial, or $6,280 with cremation (NFDA 2023).

Which funeral option costs the least in Delaware?

Direct cremation carries the lowest price tag in Delaware, from roughly $2,000. Direct burial is the next step up at about $4,300, which is $4,300 below a traditional Delaware funeral.

How can I find affordable funeral homes in Delaware?

Delaware is small enough that homes in New Castle, Kent and Sussex counties are all within a drive, so compare more than one county. Ask each for its General Price List and look at the direct cremation and immediate burial lines first. If the person was on Medicaid, TANF or General Assistance, mention the $100 DSS payment.
