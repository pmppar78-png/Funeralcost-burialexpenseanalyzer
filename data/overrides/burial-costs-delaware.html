Delaware burial oversight and where to get help

Funeral homes handling burials in Delaware are licensed by the Delaware Division of Professional Regulation (302-744-4500). The Delaware Cemetery Board in the Division of Public Health keeps a cemetery registration and runs the Distressed Cemetery Fund, but it has no enforcement power (29 Del. C. §§ 7901A and 7904A); it refers consumer complaints elsewhere, including to the Department of Justice's Consumer Protection Unit (800-220-5424).

No funeral director is required for a Delaware burial; the Funeral Consumers Alliance does not list Delaware among the nine states that insist on one. A family can work out the plot, the grave opening and the container with the cemetery directly. Once the permit is issued the burial can proceed, and cremation has no set waiting period, though it too needs a permit (16 Del. C. § 3159).

Public help with burial costs

For someone who was on TANF, General Assistance or Medicaid, Delaware DSS pays up to $100 toward burial, sent to the funeral vendor, and a separate DSS service buries the indigent or unclaimed at public expense. (Source: 16 Del. Admin. Code 2000-2020; Delaware DSS indigent burial service.) The $255 Social Security payment goes only to a surviving spouse or child and is far below the $4,200 plot we estimate for Delaware.

A veteran's plot and marker in a national cemetery cost nothing. Delaware has two state veterans cemeteries: Delaware Veterans Memorial Cemetery in New Castle County (302-834-8046) and in Sussex County (302-934-5653) (source: VA National Cemetery Administration).

Steps to take in Delaware

  1. Price at least two cemeteries and two funeral homes; the plot, the grave opening and the marker are each priced on their own.
  2. Find out whether the cemetery requires an outer burial container before you buy one.
  3. Ask the Division of Social Services whether its indigent burial service applies before anyone buys a plot.
  4. For a veteran, ask the New Castle County or Sussex County veterans cemetery about eligibility before paying for a private grave.
  5. If a funeral home misleads you on burial prices, call 302-744-4500; there is no charge for the complaint.
