Rhode Island burial regulators and help with costs

A Rhode Island family may arrange a burial without a funeral director once the death certificate is filed and a disposition permit is issued. There is no 24-hour wait for burial; that clock and the medical examiner's cremation certificate apply only to cremation (R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-3-18). Funeral homes are licensed through the Board of Funeral Directing/Embalming at the Rhode Island Department of Health.

GPA-Burial

For a Rhode Island burial, GPA-Burial adds $150 toward a lot, $175 for opening and closing and $125 for a liner the cemetery requires to its $900 funeral payment, if an application is filed within 30 days of death. Relatives may contribute up to $1,600 for additional funeral-home services and merchandise without affecting that $900 basic allowance (R.I. Gen. Laws § 40-6-3.10; 218-RICR-20-00-3; checked November 12, 2026). Social Security's $255 death payment goes to a surviving spouse or child who claims it within two years, not to the cemetery. Because the GPA amounts are fixed, get the cemetery's lot, opening and liner prices first and see how far they exceed what the program pays.

Rhode Island has no VA national cemetery taking burials, but eligible veterans can be buried at the state's Rhode Island Veterans Memorial Cemetery (401-268-3088), where the plot and marker are free (source: VA National Cemetery Administration).

Next steps in Rhode Island

  1. Ask the cemetery for its own price sheet, separate from the funeral home's General Price List, and compare at least two.
  2. Find out whether the cemetery requires a liner, because GPA-Burial pays its $125 only then.
  3. For a family plot, check the town's burial-ground ordinance before anything else.
  4. For a veteran, call the Memorial Cemetery with the DD214 discharge record ready.
  5. Price complaints about a funeral home go to the board at 401-222-4998.
