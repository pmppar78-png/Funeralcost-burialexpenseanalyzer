Maine's board, town assistance and veterans cemeteries

Maine requires a 48-hour wait before cremation (32 M.R.S. § 1405). A family does not have to hire a funeral director for after-death care, but someone still has to file the death certificate and obtain the disposition permit. Funeral directors and establishments are licensed by the Maine Board of Funeral Service.

Town and city help

Burial help in Maine comes from the municipality, not the county. Under 22 M.R.S. § 4313, a town or city's general assistance program may pay the funeral director's direct costs when no legally liable relative can, up to $1,620 for a burial and $1,125 for a cremation, the maxima in the latest Appendix H posted on the official DHHS operating-guidance page (the document is labeled October 1, 2025 through September 30, 2026; as of November 12, 2026 a successor appendix had not been posted — confirm the figure in force with the town office). (Source: 22 M.R.S. § 4313; Maine DHHS, GA operating guidance, Appendix H.) The $255 from Social Security is for an eligible spouse or child who claims within two years, and never to the Maine funeral home.

Veterans cemeteries in Maine

For Acadia National Cemetery (508-563-7113), your Maine funeral home books through the VA's National Cemetery Scheduling Office, 800-535-1117. The state and tribal cemeteries take requests themselves: Central Maine Veterans Cemetery (207-287-3481), Northern Maine Veterans Cemetery (207-492-0577), Southern Maine Veterans Cemetery (207-490-4612) and Houlton Band of Maliseet Indians Veterans Cemetery (tribal, 207-532-4273).

Maine steps

  1. Ask each Maine home for its General Price List, which the FTC Funeral Rule requires it to give anyone asking in person.
  2. A memorial after the cremation or burial lets a family in Maine skip embalming and viewing charges.
  3. Talk to the town or city general assistance administrator before the funeral; it pays only the funeral director's direct costs.
  4. For a veteran, ask the funeral home about Acadia National Cemetery or a state veterans cemetery before buying a plot.
  5. If a Maine home won't show or follow its price list, tell the board: 207-624-8624.
