Who licenses Indiana funeral homes, and the help available

Indiana will not allow a cremation until 48 hours after death unless a city or county health officer waives that wait in writing (IC 23-14-31-36). That delay is the one that most often shapes a funeral followed by cremation. A licensed funeral director must also remove and transport the body (Ind. Code § 16-37); other steps, from washing and dressing the body to a service in the family home, can still be done by the family.

Directors and funeral establishments are licensed by the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency, which staffs the State Board of Funeral and Cemetery Service. That board also oversees two consumer funds, the Preneed Consumer Protection Fund and the Consumer Protection Fund for Cemetery Maintenance.

Medicaid funeral claims through FSSA

For an Indiana Medicaid recipient, FSSA pays up to $1,200 toward the funeral and up to $800 toward the cemetery, provided the claim is filed within 90 days of death. (Source: Ind. Code § 12-14-17 and the Indiana FSSA Burial Assistance Program; checked November 12, 2026.)

Veterans cemeteries in Indiana

Marion National Cemetery (765-674-0284) is the only Indiana national cemetery still accepting casket burials. Crown Hill National Cemetery Annex (317-916-5460) and New Albany National Cemetery (502-893-3852) take cremated remains only. The funeral home books any of the three through the VA's National Cemetery Scheduling Office (800-535-1117), while the state's Indiana Veterans Memorial Cemetery (812-273-9220) handles its own requests.

Indiana steps

  1. Collect itemized price lists from Indiana homes, by phone or in person; under the FTC Funeral Rule you pay only for what you pick.
  2. Ask directly whether your plans need embalming at all.
  3. Talk to the Division of Family Resources about an FSSA funeral claim and file it inside 90 days.
  4. For a veteran, have the home check Marion National Cemetery before you pay for a private plot.
  5. If a home hides its price list or will not honor it, call the board at 317-234-3031.
