Maine's board, town help and veterans cemeteries

A Maine cremation cannot take place in the first 48 hours after death unless a contagious disease caused it, and the medical examiner must certify the death first (32 M.R.S. § 1405). The same permit rule covers natural organic reduction: the state registrar or a town clerk may issue a permit only after the medical examiner's certificate (22 M.R.S. § 2843). An autopsy report from the medical examiner is free to the next of kin; other requesters pay $35 (Maine Office of Chief Medical Examiner). Crematories and funeral homes are licensed by the Maine Board of Funeral Service.

Help from the town

In Maine, burial and cremation help comes from the town or city, not the county. Municipal general assistance can pay up to $1,125 toward a cremation, limited to the funeral director's direct costs and paid only when no legally liable relative can cover them. That is the cremation maximum in the latest official Appendix H (labeled October 1, 2025 through September 30, 2026; as of November 12, 2026 a successor appendix had not been posted on the DHHS operating-guidance page, so confirm the figure in force with the town office). (Source: 22 M.R.S. § 4313; Maine DHHS, GA operating guidance, Appendix H.) Social Security's $255 death payment to a qualifying spouse or child would cover only part of the $1,900 direct cremation estimate.

Cremation leaves a Maine veteran's burial benefit intact: a niche or grave and a marker in a national cemetery are free. Acadia National Cemetery (508-563-7113) serves Maine, the state runs Central (207-287-3481), Northern (207-492-0577) and Southern (207-490-4612) Maine Veterans Cemeteries, and the Houlton Band of Maliseet Indians has a tribal veterans cemetery (207-532-4273); see cem.va.gov.

Maine checklist

  1. Ask providers to itemize transport, the container and the crematory fee.
  2. Plan the cremation date for at least 48 hours after death, plus time for the certificate and permit.
  3. If general assistance may help, have the town administrator approve the amount before services.
  4. For a winter death, ask the cemetery when it can inter an urn.
  5. Report misstated prices to the board at 207-624-8624.
