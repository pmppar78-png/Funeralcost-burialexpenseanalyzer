Wyoming cremation oversight and help with the cost

Wyoming statutes set no waiting period before cremation, but Board rules still require identification of the remains, a permit and a signed authorization before the crematory may begin. A family that has those papers may deal with a crematory itself. Crematories and the funeral homes that arrange cremation are licensed by the Wyoming State Board of Funeral Service Practitioners.

State help with the cost

The Department of Family Services pays up to $1,500 toward the cremation of a person who was on POWER, SSI or Medicaid and left too little to pay, after other resources are used, and up to $500 more to carry a qualifying veteran to the veterans cemetery. County commissioners cover other people without means (W.S. 42-2-103(c); W.S. 18-3-504(c); Wyoming DFS Burial Benefits). A surviving spouse or child who qualifies can also claim Social Security's $255, a small share of the $1,600 we estimate for a direct cremation.

An eligible Wyoming veteran's ashes can be placed at Cheyenne National Cemetery (307-778-7316) or Oregon Trail State Veterans Cemetery in Evansville (307-235-6673) with no charge for the space; see cem.va.gov.

Wyoming checklist

  1. Get the transfer and any mileage charge in writing along with the crematory fee and container.
  2. Plan for the 24-hour rule and the permit when you set a memorial date.
  3. If the person was on POWER, SSI or Medicaid, ask DFS to confirm its payment before you book.
  4. Keep the Board's number, 307-777-5403, for a complaint.
