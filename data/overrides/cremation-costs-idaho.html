Cremation rules and help in Idaho

Idaho crematories cannot start work until the coroner has given written authorization and a permit has been issued; there is no fixed waiting period after that (IDAPA 24.08.01.100; Idaho Code § 39-268). A family that has those papers can deal with a crematory itself. Cremation providers are licensed by the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses through the State Board of Morticians (DOPL Morticians).

Idaho counties must provide an indigent cremation or burial under Idaho Code § 31-3412, and many, Canyon County among them, set a fixed cremation rate by resolution. (Source: Idaho Code § 31-3412.) Social Security's $255 lump sum is paid only to an eligible Idaho spouse or child, and only if claimed within two years of the death.

Cremated remains of an eligible Idaho veteran can be placed in a VA national cemetery columbarium or in-ground site at no charge for the space. Options in Idaho include Snake River Canyon National Cemetery (208-732-7499), Idaho State Veterans Cemetery at Blackfoot (208-701-7161), and Idaho State Veterans Cemetery at Boise (208-780-1340). See cem.va.gov.

Next steps for a cremation in Idaho

  1. Ask Idaho cremation providers to itemize transport, the container and the crematory fee before you choose one.
  2. Confirm who signs the Idaho cremation permit and when, so the memorial date is realistic.
  3. Ask the Idaho county what its negotiated cremation rate is before booking a crematory.
  4. A cremation provider that misstates its prices can be reported at 208-334-3233.
