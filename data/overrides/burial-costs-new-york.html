New York burial regulators and public help

A New York family cannot handle a burial on its own. Under N.Y. Pub. Health Law § 4140, a licensed funeral director files the death certificate, moves the body, obtains the permit and supervises the disposition. The cemetery opens and closes the grave. Funeral homes are licensed by the State Department of Health's Bureau of Funeral Directing. Not-for-profit cemetery corporations answer to the Division of Cemeteries, while religious, municipal and family cemeteries do not.

If plans change to cremation, New York sets no fixed waiting period, but a permit is required (Pub. Health Law 4145; N-PCL 1517).

Help with the cost

In New York City, HRA can pay up to $1,700 toward a burial for a low-income City resident. If the funeral bill exceeds $3,400, the cost of the plot or grave opening is deducted from the total; if what remains is less than $3,400, an allowance can still be approved. The application must be completed within 120 days of the death. Elsewhere, the county social-services district sets the amount; ask whether its limit counts the cemetery charges (NYC HRA, Burial Assistance; checked November 12, 2026).

Veterans

Calverton (631-727-5410), Bath (607-664-4853), Gerald B. H. Solomon Saratoga (518-581-9128) and Western New York (585-297-1300) National Cemeteries take burials; Long Island (631-454-4949), St. Albans (718-298-8545) and Woodlawn (607-732-5411) take cremated remains only. The state runs the New York State Veterans Cemetery - Finger Lakes (607-379-0197). An eligible veteran's grave, opening and marker cost nothing (VA National Cemetery Administration).

New York burial checklist

  1. Get the funeral home's price list and the cemetery's filed or written prices before agreeing to anything.
  2. Ask the cemetery whether a headstone from a monument dealer is allowed, and what foundation fee applies.
  3. Outside the city, ask the social-services district about its burial limit.
  4. For a veteran, have the DD214 ready when you call Calverton or another national cemetery.
  5. Report a funeral home to 518-402-0785, or a not-for-profit cemetery to 518-474-6226.
