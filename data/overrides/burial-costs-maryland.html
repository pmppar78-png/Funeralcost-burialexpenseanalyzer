Burial rules and help in Maryland

If a Maryland family later chooses cremation instead of burial, the wait is only 12 hours after death (Md. Health-Gen. § 5-503), a shorter delay than most states impose. A burial itself has no such wait. Families may handle a burial without a funeral director, provided they have the burial permit and follow the cemetery's own rules for the grave.

Funeral homes are licensed by the Maryland Department of Health. Cemeteries can have a separate regulator.

For a Maryland burial, the $650 state payment survives if family or friends buy the plot, vault and grave digging, since those are left out of the $2,500 limit (COMAR 07.03.20.03). (Source: COMAR 07.03.20.03.) A surviving spouse or child may claim Social Security's one-time $255 death payment within two years; it is paid to them, not to the Maryland cemetery.

For Maryland veterans, the plot and marker in a national cemetery are free, which can remove thousands from a burial bill. In Maryland, VA national cemeteries accepting burials: Baltimore National Cemetery (410-644-9696, cremated remains only); state veterans cemeteries: Cheltenham Veterans Cemetery (301-372-6398); Crownsville Veterans Cemetery (410-987-6320); Eastern Shore Veterans Cemetery (410-943-3420); Garrison Forest Veterans Cemetery (410-363-6090); Rocky Gap Veterans Cemetery (301-777-2185) (source: VA National Cemetery Administration).

Next steps for a burial in Maryland

  1. Price the cemetery and the funeral home separately in Maryland: the plot, grave opening and marker come from the cemetery, so get at least two quotes for each.
  2. Ask whether the Maryland cemetery lets you buy the headstone elsewhere.
  3. Have family pay the plot or vault directly in Maryland so it stays outside the $2,500 Burial Assistance limit.
  4. For a veteran, call Crownsville Veterans Cemetery first and ask which documents it wants; the DD214 discharge record is normally one of them.
  5. A Maryland funeral home that misstates burial prices can be reported to the state board at 410-764-4792.
