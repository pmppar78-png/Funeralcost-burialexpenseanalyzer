Colorado burial regulators and public help

A funeral director is not required for a Colorado burial. The family can deal with the cemetery directly once it has the disposition permit. Funeral homes register with the Department of Regulatory Agencies. Starting January 1, 2027, Senate Bill 24-173 (C.R.S. 12-135-501) will also require individual licenses for directors, embalmers and cremationists, with a provisional license in the meantime. Cemeteries may have a separate regulator.

Help with the cost

Colorado Burial Assistance pays the provider at most $1,500 when the person was enrolled, at death, in a Health First Medicaid program (including Medicare Savings Programs) or in Adult Financial assistance, and only when the entire funeral and burial bill is $2,500 or less. Life insurance and voluntary contributions from a spouse or parent reduce the state payment (Colorado DHS Adult Financial Reference Guide, Burial Assistance Program, December 2025, Version 11.0; checked November 12, 2026). The $255 Social Security payment, paid only to a surviving spouse or child, would not cover the $3,800 plot we estimate for Colorado.

At a VA national cemetery, an eligible veteran's grave, burial and headstone come without charge. Colorado has three VA national cemeteries taking burials: Fort Logan (303-761-0117), Fort Lyon (719-391-6920) and Pikes Peak (719-391-6920), plus the state's Veterans Memorial Cemetery of Western Colorado (970-263-8986) (source: VA National Cemetery Administration).

What to do next in Colorado

  1. Ask each cemetery for its grave, opening, liner and marker charges as a separate quote.
  2. At elevation, check whether graves are opened in winter.
  3. For burial on private land, plan to record the location with the county within 30 days.
  4. Ask the county human services office whether the $2,500 Burial Assistance limit counts the plot and opening before choosing a cemetery.
  5. For a veteran, ask Fort Logan National Cemetery about eligibility before buying a private plot.
  6. If a funeral home misleads you about burial costs, a complaint call to 303-894-7800 is free.
