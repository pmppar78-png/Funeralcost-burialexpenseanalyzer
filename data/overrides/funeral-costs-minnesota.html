Who licenses Minnesota funeral homes, and county and veterans help

In Minnesota, funeral homes are licensed by the Department of Health's Health Regulation Division, not a separate board. Call 651-201-4200 if a home will not show its prices or leans on you to buy more.

Minnesota families may handle much of the after-death work themselves; the Funeral Consumers Alliance does not place the state among the nine where a director is mandatory for some steps. The death record and disposition permit still have to be filed. Cremation requires that permit but no waiting period (Minn. Stat. § 149A.95, subd. 6a). Minnesota sets a deadline instead: final disposition within 14 days of death or of the coroner's release, with embalming, refrigeration or dry ice once it will take longer than 72 hours (Minn. Stat. § 149A.94). A person can also name a designee for final arrangements in a health care directive, and that choice outranks next of kin (Minn. Stat. § 149A.80). Funeral homes file the death record through the state's electronic system, MR&CS.

County burial help

Counties must pay for the burial or cremation of a person who dies without the money for it (Minn. Stat. § 261.035), and each county sets its own amount. (Source: Minn. Stat. § 261.035.) Ask the county human-services office for the figure in writing before the home sets its price.

Veterans' cemeteries

Burial at Fort Snelling National Cemetery in Minneapolis (612-726-1127) is booked by the funeral director, who calls the VA's National Cemetery Scheduling Office on 800-535-1117. Families deal directly with the state cemeteries at Duluth (218-520-0120), Little Falls (320-616-2527), Preston (507-765-7320) and Redwood Falls (507-709-4390), and with the tribal Leech Lake Veterans Cemetery at Cass Lake (218-335-4463).

Minnesota steps

  1. Request each Minnesota home's printed price list; federal law says you get one on the spot.
  2. Decide on embalming and a viewing early, since dropping them saves the most.
  3. Ring the county human-services office before signing to learn its § 261.035 amount.
  4. For a veteran, have the home check Fort Snelling or the nearest state cemetery first.
  5. Report price-list problems to the Health Regulation Division, 651-201-4200.
