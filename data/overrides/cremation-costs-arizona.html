Who regulates Arizona cremation, and where to find help

Arizona sets no waiting period before cremation once the required permit is issued (A.R.S. § 32-1365.02). A family may deal with a crematory itself after that permit is in hand. Arizona also licenses alkaline hydrolysis, sometimes sold as water cremation: no one may run or advertise a hydrolysis facility without a separate state license (A.R.S. § 32-1301, § 32-1341). If the person with the right to authorize cannot be contacted, or will not make arrangements within 15 days of the provider's first contact, the provider may treat that person as unavailable and take consent from the next person in line (A.R.S. § 32-1365.02). Cremation providers are licensed by the Arizona Department of Health Services, Bureau of Licensing for Professions & Occupations.

If the family cannot pay

Each Arizona county has an indigent program, usually run by the public fiduciary, that handles the disposition only after family members decline or cannot pay (A.R.S. § 36-831); several counties, including Yavapai, provide it as a cremation. (Source: A.R.S. §§ 36-831, 11-251(27).) The $255 Social Security death payment goes only to a qualifying spouse or child and covers part of the $1,600 direct cremation estimate above.

A cremated Arizona veteran has the same burial benefit as one who is buried: the niche or plot and the marker in a national cemetery are free. Arizona options include the National Memorial Cemetery of Arizona (480-513-3600), Prescott National Cemetery (928-445-4860, cremated remains only) and the Arizona Veterans Memorial Cemetery at Camp Navajo (928-214-3474). See cem.va.gov.

Next steps in Arizona

  1. Get written direct cremation prices from two or more Arizona providers and compare exactly what each includes.
  2. Find out which office signs cremation permits where the death occurred, and how long its review usually takes.
  3. If the public fiduciary may be involved, ask what financial records it wants, such as bank statements, before arranging the cremation.
  4. Write down BLPO's number, 602-364-2079, before paying any cremation provider.
