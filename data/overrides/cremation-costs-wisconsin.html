Wisconsin's regulator, WFCAP help and veterans cemeteries

Wisconsin requires a 48-hour wait before cremation unless the death was from a contagious disease, plus a permit from the coroner or medical examiner (Wis. Stat. § 979.10). Once those conditions are met, a family may arrange the cremation without hiring a funeral director. Crematories and funeral establishments are licensed by the Department of Safety and Professional Services.

WFCAP

The Wisconsin Funeral and Cemetery Aids Program pays up to $1,000 toward the crematory's charges and up to $1,500 toward the funeral home's, but only when the total bill stays inside the program's limits. Ask the county or tribal WFCAP agency before choosing a package. (Source: Wisconsin DHS.)

A Wisconsin veteran who is cremated keeps the cemetery benefit, and urns can be placed in national cemeteries free. Options include Northwoods National Cemetery (906-396-9276), Central Wisconsin Veterans Memorial Cemetery (715-256-5000) and Northern Wisconsin Veterans Memorial Cemetery (715-635-5360); see cem.va.gov.

Wisconsin checklist

  1. Ask whether death certificates and the permit fee are part of the quote.
  2. Allow for the 48-hour wait under Wis. Stat. 979.10 in any travel or memorial plans.
  3. Confirm the $1,000 crematory amount with the WFCAP agency before booking.
  4. Report misstated prices to the department at 608-266-2112.
