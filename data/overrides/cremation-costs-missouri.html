Missouri's board, county-paid cremation and veterans cemeteries

Missouri has no fixed waiting period before cremation. What it requires is written authorization from the next of kin, the coroner or the person who died (20 CSR 2120-2.071; RSMo § 194.119), and a completed death certificate filed with the local registrar of the county where the death happened (RSMo 193.175). The State Board of Embalmers & Funeral Directors licenses Missouri crematories and the funeral homes that sell cremation.

When no one claims the body

A Missouri coroner may arrange the cremation of an unclaimed body, and the county commission reimburses the reasonable actual cost (RSMo § 58.460). (Source: RSMo §§ 58.460, 58.550.) Families should also know that Social Security pays its $255 death benefit to an eligible spouse or child, not to the provider, and only on a claim filed within two years.

A Missouri veteran's national cemetery benefit survives cremation, with the niche or plot and the marker free. In Missouri the choices include Jefferson Barracks National Cemetery (314-845-8320), Springfield National Cemetery (417-881-9499, cremated remains only) and the Missouri State Veterans Cemetery at Bloomfield (573-568-3871); see cem.va.gov.

Missouri checklist

  1. Check whether the death certificates and permit fee are in the quote or added later.
  2. Find out who will sign the authorization and the permit, and when, before you fix a memorial date.
  3. For an unclaimed body, ask the coroner whether the county commission will reimburse the cremation.
  4. If a provider's bill does not match its price list, call the board at 573-751-0813.
