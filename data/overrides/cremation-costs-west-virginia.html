West Virginia cremation oversight and public help

West Virginia does not require a funeral director for every after-death step, and it sets no waiting period before cremation, but no crematory will start without written next-of-kin permission and a cremation permit from a medical examiner or coroner (W. Va. Code § 30-6-21). If no one collects the ashes, the funeral board's timetable requires the provider to write by certified mail 60 days after the cremation, write again 30 days later, and warn that it may dispose of the ashes if they are still unclaimed 90 days after that (WV Board of Funeral Service Examiners). At the arrangement conference the funeral home must also tell the family in writing that unclaimed remains may go into a common grave and may not be recoverable later (W. Va. Code R. § 6-1-24). Funeral homes and cremation providers are licensed by the West Virginia Board of Funeral Service Examiners.

When money is short

West Virginia's Indigent Burial program pays a $1,000 rate for a direct cremation, but only while the resources and family payments the funeral home receives stay below $2,200; the first $1,200 of those does not count against the rate. (Source: W. Va. Code § 9-5-18; WV Funeral Home Director Handbook (Indigent Burial).) The $255 Social Security lump sum goes only to an eligible spouse or child and must be claimed within two years of the death.

A veteran keeps the cemetery benefit after cremation: an urn can be interred in a national cemetery at no charge. West Virginia options include West Virginia National Cemetery (304-265-2044) and the Donel C. Kinnard Memorial State Veterans Cemetery (304-746-0026). See cem.va.gov.

Next steps in West Virginia

  1. Ask two or more West Virginia providers for written direct cremation prices and check what each one covers.
  2. Find out who will sign the cremation permit and how soon, so the memorial date holds.
  3. Tell the DoHS worker about any family money paid to the funeral home; more than $1,200 reduces the $1,000 cremation rate.
  4. Report a provider that misstates its prices to the Board at 304-558-0302.
