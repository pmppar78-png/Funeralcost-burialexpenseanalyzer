Hawaii cremation oversight and state help

Hawaii sets no waiting period before cremation; the health department permit waits only for the filed death certificate (HRS §§ 338-23, 338-25). A family may work with a crematory itself once that permit is issued. Complaints about a cemetery or a prepaid funeral plan seller go to the Regulated Industries Complaints Office of the Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs.

Unclaimed remains

When no one claims a body, Hawaii has it cremated, and DHS pays the provider its cost or $1,600, whichever is lower, if the provider applies within 60 days of death (Haw. Rev. Stat. § 346-15; HAR ch. 17-1745). Social Security's $255 goes only to an eligible spouse or child who claims within two years.

Veterans

Urns are accepted at the Hawaii State Veterans Cemetery (808-369-3575), the National Memorial Cemetery of the Pacific (808-532-3720, cremated remains only) and Kauai Veterans Cemetery (808-241-4460), with the niche or grave and marker free for an eligible veteran; see cem.va.gov.

Hawaii checklist

  1. Get at least two written direct cremation prices and compare what each covers.
  2. Ask when the permit will issue, and whether refrigeration is charged after 30 hours.
  3. For a scattering at sea, decide who files the EPA report.
  4. Report misquoted cemetery or prepaid-plan prices to 808-586-2670.
