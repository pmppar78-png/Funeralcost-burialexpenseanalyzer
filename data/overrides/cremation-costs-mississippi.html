Mississippi cremation rules and public help

Mississippi sets no waiting period before cremation, but the death certificate must be completed and filed with Vital Records before the cremation can begin (15 Miss. Admin. Code Pt. 5, R. 4.7.2). A family may work with a crematory itself once that filing is done. Cremation providers are licensed by the Mississippi State Board of Funeral Service.

When the family cannot pay

Each county's board of supervisors may pay some or all of an indigent person's disposition, following the burial policy recorded in its minutes (Miss. Code Ann. § 43-31-29), so ask whether that policy includes cremation. (Source: Miss. Code Ann. § 43-31-29.) Social Security adds only $255, payable to a spouse or child who applies within two years.

Cremation also leaves a veteran's cemetery benefit intact, and the urn is placed in a national cemetery free. Mississippi has Biloxi National Cemetery (228-388-6668), Corinth National Cemetery (901-386-8311) and Natchez National Cemetery (601-445-4981). See cem.va.gov.

What to do next for a Mississippi cremation

  1. Ask each provider to list transport, the container and the crematory fee separately.
  2. Check for the $25 medical examiner certificate on the itemized bill.
  3. Find out who signs the cremation permit and when, before fixing a memorial date.
  4. Ask the county supervisors whether they pay the whole cremation or only part.
  5. Pricing complaints go to the Board of Funeral Service at 601-932-1973.
