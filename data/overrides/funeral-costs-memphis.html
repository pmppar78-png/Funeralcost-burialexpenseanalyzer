Memphis funerals: Tennessee's board, Shelby County aid and the West Tennessee veterans cemeteries

Tennessee does not require a Memphis family to hire a funeral director for most after-death steps, and it sets no waiting period before cremation, though the cremation permit and a signed authorization are still required. Memphis funeral homes are licensed by the Tennessee Board of Funeral Directors and Embalmers.

Help for a family without money depends on Shelby County. Tennessee lets each county's legislative body decide whether it will pay for the burial or cremation of a poor person, and any claim must come with a sworn statement of the cost and of the lack of other means (Tenn. Code Ann. § 5-9-101(4); § 5-9-311). Ask the Shelby County mayor's office how the county handles this before you commit to a funeral home. Social Security's $255 death payment, limited to a qualifying spouse or child, covers only a sliver of the $7,035 Tennessee average in the table. Tennessee does set one floor: when the medical examiner cannot release a body to relatives, the body must be decently buried or cremated, with the cost taken from property found with it or, failing that, paid from the county treasury (Tenn. Att'y Gen. Op. 18-13, citing Tenn. Code Ann. § 38-5-118).

Memphis has its own national cemetery, but Memphis National Cemetery (901-386-8311) now takes cremated remains only. For a casket burial a funeral home can book Chattanooga National Cemetery (423-855-6590) or Mountain Home National Cemetery (423-979-3535); Knoxville (423-979-3535) and Nashville (615-860-0086) national cemeteries are also limited to cremated remains. All are scheduled through the VA's National Cemetery Scheduling Office at 800-535-1117. Closer to home, West Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery (901-543-7005) and the Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery at Parkers Crossroads (731-967-4127) take requests directly, as do East Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery (865-577-3228) and Middle Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery (615-532-2238).

Before you sign with a Memphis funeral home

  1. Get printed price lists from at least two Memphis homes and compare them line by line.
  2. Ask the Shelby County mayor's office whether the county pays for indigent burials.
  3. A memorial service after cremation or burial avoids embalming and viewing charges.
  4. For a veteran, check the West Tennessee state cemetery before buying a private plot.
  5. Report a home that will not honor its price list to the board at 615-741-5062.
