Indiana's licensing agency, FSSA help and veterans cemeteries

Funeral homes that arrange Indiana burials are licensed through the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency (317-234-3031), which can also tell you which office takes a complaint about a cemetery.

Indiana families cannot handle a burial entirely on their own. Ind. Code § 16-37 requires a licensed funeral director to remove and transport the body, as the Funeral Consumers Alliance summarizes the law; the cemetery opens and closes the grave.

Burial has no waiting period. If the family switches to cremation, Indiana requires a 48-hour wait (IC 23-14-31-36).

Help for Medicaid recipients

Indiana FSSA help for a Medicaid recipient can pay up to $1,200 to the funeral home and up to $800 to the cemetery, and the claim must be filed within 90 days (Ind. Code § 12-14-17). (Source: Ind. Code § 12-14-17.) A surviving spouse or child may also claim Social Security's $255 within two years.

Veterans

Eligible veterans receive a grave, opening and closing, a liner and a headstone free in a national cemetery. Indiana's choices are Marion National Cemetery (765-674-0284), Crown Hill National Cemetery Annex (317-916-5460, cremated remains only), New Albany National Cemetery (502-893-3852, cremated remains only) and the state's Indiana Veterans Memorial Cemetery (812-273-9220) (source: VA National Cemetery Administration). For a casket burial, Marion is the only national cemetery in Indiana. The Crown Hill annex is in Indianapolis, New Albany faces Louisville across the Ohio River, and the state cemetery is in Madison.

Indiana checklist

  1. Price the cemetery on its own, since the plot, opening and closing, vault or liner and marker are often sold apart from the funeral.
  2. Ask whether the cemetery requires an outer burial container before shopping for one.
  3. Send the cemetery's bill for the $800 share before the 90-day FSSA deadline.
  4. For a veteran, ask Marion National Cemetery what it needs; the DD214 discharge record usually comes first.
  5. Call 317-234-3031 if a funeral home misprices a burial.
