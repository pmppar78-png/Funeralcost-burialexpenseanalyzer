Virginia's boards, local burial help and veterans cemeteries

The Virginia Board of Funeral Directors and Embalmers (804-367-4479) licenses the funeral homes that handle burials. Licensed cemetery companies come under the Cemetery Board instead, and church and family cemeteries usually under neither.

No funeral director is needed for a Virginia burial; the state is missing from the Funeral Consumers Alliance list of nine that demand one. That lets a family deal with a Virginia cemetery directly about the plot, opening the grave and which container it accepts.

Burial has no waiting period. If the family decides on cremation instead, Virginia sets no fixed wait either, but a permit is needed (Va. Code 54.1-2818.1).

Help with the cost

When a resident's body is unclaimed, the Virginia city or county pays the reasonable cost of burial, with a court's approval (Va. Code § 32.1-309.2). (Source: Va. Code §§ 32.1-309.1, 32.1-309.2.) Social Security's $255 for a surviving spouse or child is a small part of the $4,000 plot estimate on this page.

Veterans

For a qualifying veteran, the grave, opening and closing, liner and headstone in a national cemetery are free. Virginia's VA national cemeteries taking burials are Culpeper (540-825-0027), Danville (704-636-2661, cremated remains only) and the National Memorial Cemetery at Quantico (703-221-2183). The state also runs the Albert G. Horton, Jr. Memorial Veterans Cemetery, the Southwest Virginia Veterans Cemetery and the Virginia Veterans Cemetery in Amelia, all reached at 855-482-8387 (source: VA National Cemetery Administration).

Next steps in Virginia

  1. Ask the cemetery for its General Price List; a licensed Virginia cemetery company has to provide one.
  2. Find out the cemetery's rules on opening times, containers and markers.
  3. For an unclaimed body, ask the city or county whether a court has approved the burial before buying any plot.
  4. For a veteran, call Quantico about eligibility before paying for a private grave.
  5. Report a funeral home that misleads you about prices by calling 804-367-4479.
