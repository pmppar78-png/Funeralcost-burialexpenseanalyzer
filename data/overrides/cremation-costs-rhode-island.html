Cremation rules and help in Rhode Island

A Rhode Island cremation cannot start until 24 hours after death, and only after the medical examiner has issued a cremation certificate (R.I. Gen. Laws § 23-3-18). Once those conditions are met, a family may deal with a crematory itself. Cremation providers are licensed by the Board of Funeral Directing/Embalming at the Rhode Island Department of Health.

Rhode Island's GPA-Burial program pays up to $900 toward the funeral home's charges and up to $150 for the crematory fee, paid directly to the providers, when an application is filed within 30 days of death. Relatives may contribute up to $1,600 for additional funeral-home services and merchandise without affecting the $900 basic allowance (R.I. Gen. Laws § 40-6-3.10; 218-RICR-20-00-3; checked November 12, 2026). The $255 Social Security death payment to a qualifying Rhode Island spouse or child would cover only part of the $2,200 direct cremation estimate above.

Cremated remains of an eligible Rhode Island veteran can be placed in a VA national cemetery columbarium or in-ground site at no charge for the space. The one option in Rhode Island is Rhode Island Veterans Memorial Cemetery (401-268-3088). See cem.va.gov.

Next steps for a cremation in Rhode Island

  1. Ask Rhode Island cremation providers to itemize transport, the container and the crematory fee before you choose one.
  2. Allow at least 24 hours after death in Rhode Island, plus time for the permit, before the cremation date.
  3. Send the Rhode Island DHS GPA-Burial application within 30 days so the $150 crematory fee is covered.
  4. Note the board's number, 401-222-4998, before you pay a cremation provider.
