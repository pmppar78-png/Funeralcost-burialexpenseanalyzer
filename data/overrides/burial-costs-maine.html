Maine's board, general assistance and veterans cemeteries

A Maine burial can go ahead once the death certificate is filed and a disposition permit is issued; unlike cremation, burial has no 48-hour wait (32 M.R.S. § 1405). Families may arrange the burial themselves. Funeral homes that handle burials are licensed by the Maine Board of Funeral Service. A cemetery may answer to a municipality, a religious organization or a private association rather than that board.

Help with the cost

Through general assistance, a Maine town can pay up to $1,620 toward a burial. The administrator may add the actual cost of a liner the cemetery requires, opening the grave, and a lot in the cheapest section (22 M.R.S. § 4313). (Source: 22 M.R.S. § 4313; Maine DHHS, GA operating guidance, Appendix H.) If the town has its own cemetery, ask about a lot there first, because the program will not pay for a lot elsewhere. A qualifying spouse or child can also claim Social Security's $255 death payment within two years; it goes to them, not to the cemetery.

Veterans

A veteran who qualifies is buried in a national cemetery with the grave, opening and closing, liner and headstone provided free. Acadia National Cemetery (508-563-7113) is the VA national cemetery in Maine. The state runs Central Maine Veterans Cemetery (207-287-3481), Northern Maine Veterans Cemetery (207-492-0577) and Southern Maine Veterans Cemetery (207-490-4612), and the Houlton Band of Maliseet Indians operates a tribal veterans cemetery (207-532-4273) (source: VA National Cemetery Administration).

Before you commit in Maine

  1. Ask the cemetery for its own price sheet, since the plot, grave opening, liner and marker are usually billed apart from the funeral home.
  2. Check the cemetery's rules on winter burials, containers and markers.
  3. If general assistance may help, ask the town about a municipal lot before buying one anywhere else.
  4. For a veteran, find the DD214 discharge record and call Acadia National Cemetery about eligibility.
  5. If a funeral home misstates its burial prices, tell the board at 207-624-8624.
