Michigan's funeral regulator, SER burial help and veterans cemeteries

Michigan requires a licensed funeral director to file the death certificate, move the body, supervise the disposition and obtain the permits (Mich. Comp. Laws § 700.3206). The county medical examiner must also authorize a cremation (Mich. Comp. Laws § 333.2848). Funeral homes are licensed by the Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs.

State Emergency Relief burial payments

Michigan's SER program pays fixed amounts for 2026: $875 for burial with a memorial service and $610 without; $640 for cremation with a memorial and $390 without (Michigan MDHHS). Apply through MI Bridges or the local MDHHS office; the request must be made no later than 20 business days after the burial, cremation or donation, the person's remains must already be in Michigan, and the funeral home's total charge has to stay within the SER limits for the payment to be made.

Prepaid funerals

Michigan's Prepaid Funeral and Cemetery Sales Act requires money paid for a prepaid funeral to be held in escrow or covered by insurance, and sellers must be registered with LARA. Ask to see the contract's cancellation terms and who holds the funds before you pay.

Veterans

Fort Custer National Cemetery near Augusta (269-731-4164) and Great Lakes National Cemetery in Holly, northwest of Detroit (248-328-0386), are booked by the funeral home through the VA's National Cemetery Scheduling Office at 800-535-1117.

Burial transit and out-of-state deaths

When a Michigan resident dies in another state, the funeral director there files that state's death certificate and a burial-transit permit travels with the body; the certificate is ordered from the state where the death occurred, not from Michigan. If the body has to travel by common carrier and won't arrive within 48 hours of death, Michigan's transport rules call for embalming, so ask whether refrigeration and a direct drive are possible instead.

Michigan checklist

  1. Get a General Price List from each home; it must be given to you on request.
  2. Make the embalming and viewing decision first.
  3. Apply for SER promptly; MDHHS requires the request no later than 20 business days after the burial, cremation or donation, and the remains must be in Michigan.
  4. For a veteran, ask about Great Lakes or Fort Custer before buying a plot.
  5. Complaints about a home's prices go to LARA, 517-241-9221.
