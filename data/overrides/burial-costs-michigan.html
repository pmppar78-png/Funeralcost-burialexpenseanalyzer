Michigan burial oversight, SER and veterans' graves

A Michigan burial still requires a licensed funeral director to file the death certificate, move the body, supervise the disposition and obtain the permits (Mich. Comp. Laws § 700.3206). The medical-examiner authorization in Mich. Comp. Laws § 333.2848 is a cremation rule, not a burial wait. Funeral homes are licensed by the Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs.

State Emergency Relief

For 2026, SER pays $875 toward a burial with a memorial service and $610 without one, with lower cremation amounts (Michigan MDHHS; checked November 12, 2026). Against our $16,320-plus Michigan burial total it is a small contribution, and the provider's total has to stay within SER limits. The application has to be filed no later than 20 business days after the burial, and the person's remains must already be in Michigan; MDHHS does not pay to bring someone back from another state.

Veterans

An eligible veteran's plot, opening and closing, liner and marker are free at Great Lakes National Cemetery in Holly (248-328-0386) or Fort Custer National Cemetery in Augusta, near Battle Creek (269-731-4164) (VA National Cemetery Administration). The VA lists no state-run veterans cemetery in Michigan.

Who regulates the cemetery

Private cemeteries are registered by LARA's Cemetery Commissioner under the Cemetery Regulation Act (1968 PA 251, MCL 456.521 et seq.), who audits their trust funds and investigates complaints (517-241-9221). Each must keep an irrevocable endowment and perpetual care trust and deposit at least 15% of every month's grave, crypt and niche sales, never less than $20 for a single burial-right sale (MCL 456.536); only the income may be spent, on mowing, grave repair and upkeep. Municipal and church cemeteries are exempt, so ask a township or parish cemetery how it pays for long-term care.

Before buying a Michigan plot

  1. Ask the cemetery for a written price sheet; the funeral home's list won't show its charges.
  2. Ask whether the cemetery requires a vault or will take a cheaper liner.
  3. For a veteran, ask Great Lakes or Fort Custer what papers they need; the DD214 comes first.
  4. If a funeral home's burial charges don't match its price list, complain to LARA, 517-241-9221.
