Cremation rules and help in North Carolina

A North Carolina cremation cannot start within 24 hours of death. Before the crematory proceeds, a medical examiner must certify that no further examination is needed (N.C.G.S. 90-210.129, 130A-388). The same statute gives relatives and their invitees access to the crematory area, subject to the crematory's safety rules, and it requires remains to be kept at 40 degrees Fahrenheit or colder unless the cremation will begin within 24 hours of the crematory taking custody. The crematory will also want the death certificate, the cremation permit and a signed authorization from the next of kin.

Crematories and the funeral homes that sell cremation are licensed by the North Carolina Board of Funeral Service. A family does not have to hire a director for every step, but the crematory will not skip that paperwork.

A North Carolina county DSS director arranges cremation or hydrolysis of an unclaimed body, paid by the estate first and then by the county where the person lived (N.C. Gen. Stat. § 130A-415). (Source: N.C. Gen. Stat. § 130A-415.) Social Security's $255 lump sum is paid only to an eligible North Carolina spouse or child, and only if claimed within two years of the death.

Choosing cremation does not give up a North Carolina veteran's cemetery benefit; urns can be interred in national cemeteries free of charge. Options in North Carolina include Salisbury National Cemetery Annex (704-636-2661), Coastal Carolina State Veterans Cemetery (910-347-4550), and Eastern Carolina State Veterans Cemetery (919-648-0510). See cem.va.gov.

Next steps for a cremation in North Carolina

  1. Ask North Carolina cremation providers to itemize transport, the container and the crematory fee before you choose one.
  2. Build the 24-hour wait set by N.C.G.S. 90-210.129, 130A-388 into any North Carolina travel or memorial plans.
  3. Ask the North Carolina DSS director whether the county uses cremation or hydrolysis for unclaimed bodies.
  4. A cremation provider that misstates its prices can be reported at 919-733-9380.
