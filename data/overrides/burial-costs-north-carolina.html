North Carolina's burial regulators and public help

For a license check or complaint about a funeral home arranging a burial in North Carolina, call the North Carolina Board of Funeral Service at 919-733-9380; North Carolina cemeteries can have a separate regulator. For most cemeteries that regulator is the N.C. Cemetery Commission, which supervises cemeteries, their sales organizations and brokers, and preneed cemetery salespeople under Chapter 65, Article 9; cemeteries run by churches or government agencies fall outside that law.

Families in North Carolina can arrange a burial without a funeral director; the state is not one of the nine the Funeral Consumers Alliance lists as requiring one. The plot, the grave opening and the container can be arranged with the cemetery itself. Burial has no waiting period, though a switch to cremation brings a 24-hour wait (N.C.G.S. 90-210.129, 130A-388).

Paying for it

When a body is unclaimed, a North Carolina county pays for burial only after the estate has been used, and the person's residence decides which county is responsible (Source: N.C. Gen. Stat. § 130A-415). Social Security pays $255 only to a surviving spouse or child, a small fraction of the $3,200 plot we estimate for North Carolina.

A veteran's plot and marker in a national cemetery are free. North Carolina's VA national cemetery taking burials is the Salisbury National Cemetery Annex (704-636-2661), and the state runs four veterans cemeteries: Coastal Carolina (910-347-4550), Eastern Carolina (919-648-0510), Sandhills (919-664-1175) and Western Carolina (828-669-0684) (source: VA National Cemetery Administration).

A short checklist for North Carolina

  1. Get the cemetery's plot, opening and closing, vault and marker prices separately; they are often sold apart from the funeral home.
  2. Ask the cemetery about its grave opening times and its vault and marker rules.
  3. If no one can claim the body, ask the county DSS about a county-paid burial before buying a plot.
  4. For a veteran, call Salisbury National Cemetery Annex or a state veterans cemetery about eligibility before buying a private grave.
  5. If a funeral home misleads you about burial prices, a call to the Board at 919-733-9380 costs nothing.
