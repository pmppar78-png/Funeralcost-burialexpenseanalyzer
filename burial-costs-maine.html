Burial Costs in Maine (2026)

Reviewed: February 2026 Region: Northeast

Maine's burial landscape is shaped by its rugged New England character — from the ancient town cemeteries that dot its rocky coastline to the quiet pine-shaded graveyards of its inland communities. As the oldest state by median age in the nation, Maine faces growing demand for burial services, and families here navigate a market where moderate pricing blends with centuries of Yankee tradition. Maine's town cemeteries, many dating to the colonial and early republic eras, remain the cornerstone of burial in the state, while the Maine Veterans Memorial Cemetery and a small number of private memorial parks serve additional needs. Rocky soil and harsh winters present practical challenges, but they have also produced some of the most character-rich, beautifully weathered cemeteries in New England.

What Drives Burial Costs in Maine

Rocky Soil and Winter Challenges

Maine's glacially deposited rocky soil can make grave excavation more expensive, sometimes requiring specialized equipment. Winter burials may face frozen ground conditions from November through April, requiring ground-thawing equipment or temporary storage of remains until spring.

Town Cemeteries

Maine's strong town government tradition means that most burial occurs in municipally-owned town cemeteries. These are maintained through local taxes and perpetual care funds, and typically offer the most affordable plots in their communities.

Notable Maine Cemeteries

Maine Veterans Memorial Cemetery in Augusta provides free burial for eligible veterans. Evergreen Cemetery in Portland, established in 1855, is the state's largest and most historic cemetery. Togus National Cemetery serves veterans near Augusta.

Cemetery Types Available in Maine

  • Town cemeteries — The primary burial option throughout Maine. Affordable and community-maintained.
  • Veterans cemeteries — Maine Veterans Memorial Cemetery and Togus National Cemetery. Learn about veteran burial benefits
  • Private memorial parks — Limited options, primarily in southern Maine.
  • Church cemeteries — Catholic and Protestant cemeteries across the state.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does burial cost in Maine?

A traditional burial costs approximately $8,200 for the funeral service plus $4,000 for a plot. Total costs range from $15,800 to $22,260. Portland is the most expensive area.

Is a burial vault required in Maine?

No state law requires vaults. Most cemeteries require them due to Maine's freeze-thaw cycle and rocky soil.

Can you be buried in winter in Maine?

Winter burial is possible in Maine but costs more due to frozen ground. Some families choose to store remains and hold burial in spring. Ground-thawing equipment can add $500-$1,000 to winter burial costs.

What are the cheapest burial options in Maine?

Direct burial at a town cemetery in rural Maine is most affordable. Online caskets save $1,000-$4,000. Veterans qualify for free burial at Maine Veterans Memorial Cemetery.
