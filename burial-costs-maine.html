How to Reduce Burial Costs in Maine

Burial costs in Maine can be managed with smart planning:

  • Request itemized pricing from every provider: Funeral homes in Maine must provide a General Price List. Cemeteries are not required to, but most in the Maine area will share one if you ask specifically for the itemized price sheet rather than a "package."
  • Confirm winter pricing up front: Maine cemeteries frequently add frozen-ground charges from December through March, or require winter vault storage until spring interment.
  • Denominational and municipal cemeteries: Maine has one of the highest densities of nonprofit cemeteries in the country.
  • Shop outside the immediate metro: A 20-30 minute drive from Portland into a lower-cost county can reduce plot prices substantially without changing the family's ability to visit.

Regional Context for Burial in Maine

Nonprofit, municipal, and denominational cemeteries in Maine routinely undercut commercial memorial parks by $1,200-$2,400 on the plot line.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do Maine families spend on burial?

Typical Maine burials total between $18,760 and $23,260. Of that, the Maine funeral service runs about $8,200, the cemetery plot $4,000, the casket around $2,460, the vault $1,600, opening-and-closing $2,000, and a headstone $500-$5,000.

Is a burial vault required in Maine?

Burial vaults are required by most cemeteries in Maine as a matter of cemetery policy (to prevent ground settling), though they are rarely mandated by state law. Vault costs in Maine typically range from $800 to $10,000.

How can I reduce burial costs in Maine?

Direct (immediate) burial skips embalming, viewing and a ceremony at the funeral home, so it is usually the lowest-cost burial on a Maine price list. You may supply your own casket in Maine; the FTC Funeral Rule forbids a handling fee for it. A VA national cemetery burial costs eligible Maine veterans nothing for the plot, opening and closing, or headstone.

How do cemetery prices differ across Maine?

Maine cemeteries price plots, opening and closing, and vault rules separately, so ask each one for a written price list. The FTC Funeral Rule covers funeral homes, not most cemeteries, so a Maine cemetery may not be required to give an itemized price list; ask for one anyway. VA national cemeteries in Maine include Acadia National Cemetery.

What is the total cost of burial in Maine including everything?

The first answer above lists each item; in Maine, cash advance items such as certified death certificates, an obituary notice and flowers are added on top of the $18,760 to $23,260 range.

Can I choose a natural or green burial in Maine?

A growing number of Maine cemeteries now offer green burial sections (biodegradable container, no embalming, no vault), and the total cost is usually below a traditional burial. If a dedicated green burial ground is not nearby, some families in Maine use a hybrid approach (traditional plot without embalming).

Burial rules and help in Maine

Funeral homes handling burials in Maine are licensed by the Maine Board of Funeral Service (207-624-8624). Cemetery sales in Maine may fall under a different office, so ask the board who oversees the cemetery you are considering.

Because the Funeral Consumers Alliance does not count Maine among the nine states that require a funeral director, a Maine family may arrange a burial without one; the cemetery still sets its own rules for the grave, and the burial permit must be in hand.

A burial in Maine goes ahead once the permit is issued; for cremation, a 48-hour wait applies (32 M.R.S. § 1405).

Maine general assistance pays up to $1,620 for a burial, and the administrator may add the actual cost of a required liner, the grave opening, and a lot in the least expensive section (22 M.R.S. § 4313). (Source: 22 M.R.S. § 4313; Maine GA Appendix H (10/1/26–9/30/27).) A surviving spouse or child may claim Social Security's one-time $255 death payment within two years; it is paid to them, not to the Maine cemetery.

Eligible Maine veterans get a grave, opening and closing, a liner and a headstone at no cost in a national cemetery. In Maine, VA national cemeteries accepting burials: Acadia National Cemetery (508-563-7113); state and tribal veterans cemeteries: Central Maine Veterans Cemetery (207-287-3481); Houlton Band of Maliseet Indians Veterans Cemetery (tribal, 207-532-4273); Northern Maine Veterans Cemetery (207-492-0577); Southern Maine Veterans Cemetery (207-490-4612) (source: VA National Cemetery Administration).

Next steps for a burial in Maine

  1. Price the Maine cemetery separately: plot, opening and closing, vault or liner, and marker are often sold apart from the funeral home.
  2. Ask the Maine cemetery for its rules on grave opening times, vaults and markers.
  3. Ask the town whether it has a municipal cemetery lot; Maine GA will not pay for a lot elsewhere if it does.
  4. If the person served, ask Acadia National Cemetery what paperwork it needs, usually starting with the DD214 discharge record.
  5. Report a funeral home that misprices a burial to the board at 207-624-8624.
