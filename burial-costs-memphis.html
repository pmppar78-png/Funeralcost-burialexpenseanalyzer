Burial Costs in Memphis, Tennessee (2026)

By: Paul Paradis, Editor Updated: April 2026 State: Tennessee

Memphis burial pricing reveals itself in fragments. The funeral home on Poplar Avenue sends one invoice; Elmwood, Memphis Memory Gardens, or Forest Hill sends another; the monument dealer sends a third. By the time a Shelby County family has all three in hand, the true 2026 total typically lands between $13,020 and $18,281. Most of the gap between those numbers comes down to which cemetery was chosen and whether the casket came from the funeral home showroom or an independent retailer.

Those figures are the working envelope, not the ceiling or the floor. Cemetery selection alone swings the total by thousands.

The Memphis Cemetery Landscape

Elmwood Cemetery near downtown — 80 acres of historic grounds listed on the National Register — is Memphis's answer to the garden cemetery tradition, and its pricing is correspondingly higher than that of many commercial parks. Memorial Park on Poplar and Forest Hill Midtown serve larger shares of everyday interments at prices closer to the $3,150 average. R.S. Lewis & Sons and the other longtime Memphis funeral homes usually coordinate with all three; the choice is the family's, not the funeral director's.

Shelby County Disposition Paths: Elmwood, Memorial Park, Forest Hill

  • Traditional burial — Viewing, service, casket, vault, ground interment. Still the default pattern for most Memphis families.
  • Direct burial — No viewing, no embalming, no facility ceremony. Published as a separate service by nearly every Memphis funeral home.
  • Green burial — Limited within Shelby County proper but available at a handful of rural West Tennessee cemeteries.
  • Mausoleum entombment — Available at Memorial Park and Forest Hill; avoids the vault requirement and carries higher base pricing.

The Tennessee Vault Rule Is a Cemetery Rule

Tennessee statute does not require an outer burial container. Every major Memphis cemetery requires one by internal policy. A concrete grave liner usually satisfies the requirement at roughly half the vault price — ask the cemetery specifically, not the funeral home, for their current policy.

Where Memphis Families Trim the Bill

  • Church and family cemeteries. Active Black church and family-owned cemeteries remain a significant part of West Tennessee tradition and routinely quote plots 30 to 50 percent below commercial Memphis parks. Start with the deceased's home congregation before the funeral home's preferred cemetery.
  • Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery – West Tennessee. Located in Memphis, with free burial (plot, liner, marker, opening) for eligible Tennessee veterans. See veteran burial benefits.
  • Unbundle the casket. Under the FTC Funeral Rule, R.S. Lewis & Sons and every other Memphis funeral home must accept a casket from an independent retailer without a handling fee.
  • Order the marker independently. Memphis monument dealers cut and set headstones for less than the cemetery's in-house vendor. Headstone cost guide.
  • Drive outside Shelby County. Tipton and Fayette County cemeteries routinely price plots below Memphis averages.
Casket sourcing: The FTC Funeral Rule applies in Memphis. No handling fee, no refusal. Compare online casket retailers.

Answers to the Questions We Hear Most

How much does burial cost in Memphis?

A Memphis traditional burial runs between $13,020 and $18,281 all-in. Funeral service near $7,035, plot $3,150, casket $2,111, vault $1,260, opening and closing $1,575, marker $1,500 to $3,000. Elmwood Cemetery pricing pushes families toward the top of that range; church cemeteries and the Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery pull them well below.

Is a burial vault required in Memphis?

Tennessee law does not require an outer burial container. Elmwood, Memorial Park, Forest Hill, and most Memphis commercial parks require one as a matter of cemetery policy to prevent ground settlement. A concrete grave liner typically satisfies the requirement at roughly half the price of a sealed vault — confirm in writing with the specific cemetery.

What are the cheapest burial options in Memphis?

Direct burial arranged through R.S. Lewis & Sons or a comparable Memphis funeral home eliminates embalming, viewing, and most facility fees. Church and family cemeteries in West Tennessee routinely quote plots 30 to 50 percent below Memphis commercial parks. Eligible Memphis-area veterans should look at the Tennessee State Veterans Cemetery – West Tennessee, which provides plot, liner, and marker at no cost. Sourcing the casket independently saves another $1,000 or more.
