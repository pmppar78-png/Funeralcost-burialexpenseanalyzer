Cremation Costs in Arlington, Texas (2026)

By: Paul Paradis, Editor Updated: April 2026 State: Texas Cremation Rate: 48%

Arlington sits inside one of the largest and most competitive cremation markets in the country — the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex — and local pricing reflects that. At roughly $1,870 for direct cremation, Arlington is modestly above the Texas state average, which is itself held down by the absence of any state embalming requirement. Moore Funeral Home and Simpler Cremation handle a meaningful share of the Arlington-area volume, and families who cross over into Fort Worth or into southern Dallas County for price-shopping can sometimes save a few hundred dollars. What this page is really for: explaining the Texas Funeral Service Commission rules that govern the process, how Tarrant County procedures fit in, and where the quoted prices leave room that families can actually capture.

National context: the NFDA 2024 Cremation & Burial Report and the CANA 2024 Annual Report put the U.S. cremation rate near 60.5 percent, and Arlington providers operate under the oversight of the Texas Funeral Service Commission. Families ordering death certificates typically deal with the Tarrant County Vital Records in Fort Worth.

Texas Has No State Embalming Requirement. Why That Matters in Arlington.

Unlike many states, Texas does not mandate embalming before cremation under any circumstance. A body that will be cremated within a reasonable time can be held in refrigeration — and that removes a line item ($700 to $1,000) that families in other states cannot decline. Arlington funeral homes following the Texas Funeral Service Commission's rules and the federal FTC Funeral Rule must present embalming as optional and itemize the charge only if you actually choose it. When you call Arlington providers, the direct cremation price should not include embalming unless you are also holding a viewing. If it does, ask for the line item to come out.

Tarrant County Paperwork, Step by Step

  1. Death occurs. If at home without hospice, Arlington Police and the Tarrant County Medical Examiner respond.
  2. Body moves to the funeral home of the family's choice. Transportation is included in direct cremation pricing in Arlington.
  3. Legal next-of-kin signs Texas Cremation Authorization (form VS-114 counterpart).
  4. Attending physician, hospice medical director, or Tarrant County ME signs the death certificate.
  5. Funeral home files the death certificate through the Texas Electronic Registrar (TER) system.
  6. Burial-transit permit is issued by the Arlington local registrar.
  7. Cremation occurs — typically 2 to 4 business days after authorization.
  8. Ashes are returned to family or held for interment.

Where Arlington Fits in the DFW Pricing Map

The metroplex is unusual in that cremation pricing actually varies noticeably within a 30-mile radius. Direct cremation in Fort Worth (the western anchor) tends to run $50 to $200 below Arlington. Dallas proper, particularly north Dallas and Plano, trends $100 to $300 higher than Arlington because of higher overhead at larger funeral campuses. South Dallas County and Grand Prairie sometimes undercut Arlington by a small margin. For families willing to make phone calls, the 25-minute drive across the metroplex to a lower-priced provider is routinely the path to a few hundred dollars saved.

Cost-Saving Tip: You do not need to purchase an urn from the cremation provider. The FTC Funeral Rule protects your right to use any urn or container, including ones purchased independently. See our urn buying guide.

Scattering Options Near Arlington

Arlington families have several accessible scattering options. Lake Arlington and Joe Pool Lake are managed by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers; scattering requires lake-office notification and is subject to their rules. The Trinity River corridor runs through the city; private stretches allow scattering with owner permission. Farther afield, the Dinosaur Valley State Park and Cleburne State Park (both in the Fort Worth area) require advance approval from park management. Caddo National Grassland north of the metro is a less-traveled option with a free Forest Service permit. At-sea scattering in the Gulf is a three-hour drive plus a charter, typically $400 to $900 out of Galveston or Freeport.

Assistance Programs Arlington Residents Can Use

  • Tarrant County indigent burial/cremation: Administered through the Tarrant County Medical Examiner's Office when no family can be located or when the family cannot afford services.
  • Dallas County indigent cremation: Accessible to qualifying residents; Arlington families who work in or have ties to Dallas County should check eligibility.
  • Texas Crime Victims' Compensation Program: Up to $6,500 toward funeral/cremation expenses for qualifying deaths resulting from violent crime.
  • Dallas-Fort Worth National Cemetery: Free interment of cremated remains for honorably discharged veterans and eligible family members.
  • Social Security lump-sum death benefit: $255 to surviving spouse or dependent child.

Questions and Answers

How much does cremation cost in Arlington?

Cremation costs in Arlington range from $1,870 for direct cremation to $6,380 for a full funeral service followed by cremation. These costs are close to the Texas state average of $1,700 for direct cremation. Additional expenses may include urns ($50-$3,000), memorial services ($500-$2,000), and scattering fees ($200-$600).

What types of cremation are available in Arlington?

Arlington families can choose from: direct cremation ($1,870) — the most affordable option with no viewing or ceremony; cremation with memorial service — a ceremony held after cremation; and traditional cremation ($6,380) — a full funeral service before cremation. Each can be customized to fit your family's needs and budget.

Is cremation cheaper than burial in Arlington?

Yes. Direct cremation in Arlington at $1,870 saves families approximately $6,050 compared to a traditional funeral with burial. Even cremation with a full service ($6,380) costs less than traditional burial when you factor in cemetery plot, vault, and headstone expenses.

Where can I compare cremation prices in Arlington?

The cheapest path in Arlington is to phone three or four direct-cremation specialists first, then one full-service funeral home, and ask each for the itemized General Price List the FTC Funeral Rule requires. Parting.com and the Funeral Consumers Alliance chapter covering Arlington can supplement those quotes with independent comparisons.

Can I scatter ashes in Arlington?

Scattering laws in the Arlington area follow Texas state regulations. Generally, you may scatter ashes on private property with owner permission, at sea (3+ nautical miles offshore per EPA regulations), and in some designated areas. Check local ordinances in Arlington for specific rules. Alternatives include columbarium niches, burial of ashes, and memorial keepsakes.
