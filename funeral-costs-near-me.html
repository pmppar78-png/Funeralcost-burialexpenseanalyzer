    Funeral Costs Near Me: State Estimates and Real Local Prices

    By Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor · November 19, 2026

    Families searching for funeral costs near them usually want a price they can use this week, from a funeral home they might actually call. This page helps with the part we can do honestly: it maps a ZIP code or state to our 2026 planning estimates, then points you to the documents that carry real local prices.

    We do not maintain a database of funeral-home General Price Lists. Sites that do, including Parting.com and Funeralocity, can be the right next click for that comparison. What we add is a clear state baseline, a letter you can use to request a price list yourself, and the consumer rights that apply when you do.

    How to get a real local price

    Under the FTC Funeral Rule, a funeral home must give you its General Price List when you ask, including over the phone, and must give it to you to keep when you visit in person and discuss arrangements. The list is itemized, so you can decline items you do not want. Our request letter and phone script puts that request in writing. The FTC Funeral Rule guide explains the rights behind it.

    If you would rather start from a directory of homes near a ZIP code, Parting.com offers that search. We link to it because we cannot reproduce those local list prices ourselves.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can I look up the price of a specific funeral home with this tool?

    No. This lookup maps a ZIP code to a state and shows our 2026 planning estimates for that state. It does not list funeral homes or their General Price Lists. To get a real local price, ask the home for its current General Price List, or use a directory that publishes those prices.

    Are these figures official NFDA state averages?

    No. NFDA publishes national and nine-region medians from its member study, not official figures for each state. Our state numbers are site estimates, described in our methodology. The latest official national median for a funeral with viewing and burial remains $8,300 from the 2023 study.

    Why show a state number when I typed a ZIP code?

    A ZIP code tells us the state. Cities inside a state can cost more or less than that state estimate, and we do not have a surveyed price for every city. The honest next step is to request General Price Lists from homes near that ZIP.

    Is Parting.com part of this site?

    No. Parting.com is a separate service that searches funeral homes by city or ZIP and shows local prices it has collected. We link to it because that is a job we cannot do with our own data. We do not operate Parting and we do not add a markup to their search.
