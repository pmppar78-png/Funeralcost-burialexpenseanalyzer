How the Site Is Organized

The human-readable directory below mirrors the XML index but groups pages the way a reader (rather than a crawler) would look for them:

  1. Core Pages & Tools — calculator, national cost index, high-traffic overview guides.
  2. Funeral / Cremation / Burial Costs by State — one page per service type per state (150 pages total).
  3. Metro Area Cost Pages — deeper cost detail for the largest U.S. metros.
  4. Planning, Consumer Rights & Assistance — FTC Funeral Rule, planning checklists, payment assistance, veteran benefits.
  5. Insurance, Prepaid & Financing — burial insurance, final expense, prepaid plans, and payment plans.
  6. Product & Buying Guides — caskets, urns, headstones, cremation jewelry, flowers.
  7. Funeral Costs by Religion — faith-specific guidance on rituals and their typical costs.
  8. Tools, Resources & Niche Topics — printable worksheets, embeddable widget, infant/child and pet costs.
  9. About & Legal — editorial standards, privacy policy, terms, disclaimer, contact.
