    Funeral Planning Kit for Families, Hospices and Senior Centers

    By Paul Paradis, Founder & Editor · November 19, 2026

    This kit gathers the free tools on Funeral Cost Analyzer into one packet you can use or hand to a family. There is no account to create. The pages are educational and are not a substitute for a funeral director, lawyer, or clinician.

    If you work at a hospice, senior center, church, or credit union, you may print these pages or share their links with families, as long as the source URL stays visible so anyone can check for updates.

    Your plans stay with you. We don't see or store anything you write in the planner. Download it, fill it in, and keep it yourself.

    What is in the packet

    ItemWhat it is for
    End-of-life planner (PDF)A fill-in planner for service wishes, contacts, document locations, a budget worksheet, and a General Price List request. Download it and keep it yourself; we do not see or store what you type in the planner.
    Printable planning checklistDecisions, documents, and questions to take to a funeral home.
    What to do when someone diesA first-hours and first-days sequence, written for the person making calls.
    Price comparison worksheetA side-by-side sheet for two or three General Price Lists.
    GPL request letter and phone scriptA short written request and a script for asking a home for its itemized prices.
    ZIP or state cost lookupOur 2026 state planning estimates, clearly labeled as estimates, not quotes.
    Cremation vs. burial calculatorA comparison of typical package types, by region.
    Questions to askA list to keep next to the price lists so extras are not assumed.
    Embeddable cost widgetA free embed for a website or blog, with the required attribution link left in place.
    Free AI guidePlain-language questions about costs and options. It is not a funeral director or lawyer.

    How to share it

    1. Send this page URL, or print the planner, checklist, worksheet, and request letter as a short packet.
    2. Add the state lookup and the calculator for anyone who wants numbers before they call.
    3. Keep the source links. Figures and rules change, and the live pages are the ones we update.

    If you want the cost tables on your own site, the widget page has copy-and-paste embed code. The attribution link back to this site must stay in the embed so readers can reach the full methods and state pages.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who is this kit for?

    Families who are planning ahead or arranging a funeral now, and staff at hospices, senior centers, or churches who want a free packet to hand someone. Everything linked here is already on this site and can be printed or copied without creating an account.

    Do I have to leave an email to use the checklist?

    No. The printable planning checklist opens in your browser. The optional email form on some pages only sends a note if that checklist is revised, and only if you agree.

    May a hospice or senior center print and share these pages?

    Yes, for educational use with families, with a link or printed URL back to this site so the figures and rights pages can be checked for updates. Do not present the pages as medical, legal, or funeral-director advice, and do not remove the source notes.

    Does this kit include local funeral-home prices?

    The kit itself is not a funeral-home directory. It includes a ZIP-to-state estimate tool and a letter for requesting a General Price List. We also keep a small, dated sample of lists that funeral homes posted in five cities, each linked to the home's own list.
